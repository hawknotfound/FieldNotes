# Budget Travel for Student Events

Practical tips on traveling cheaply within India for hackathons, competitions, and conferences.

---

## Transportation

### Trains (recommended for most trips)

- **Sleeper class** — cheapest, ₹200-600 for most routes
- **3AC** — comfortable, ₹600-1500 for medium distances
- **Student concession** — 40-50% discount on sleeper/3AC
  - Requires a Student Concession Certificate from your college
  - Valid for specific journeys related to education/academic activities

**Tips:**
- Book at least 30-60 days in advance for confirmed tickets
- Use IRCTC website/app
- Tatkal booking opens one day before — use only if urgent (more expensive)
- Overnight trains save accommodation cost

### Buses

| Type | Cost (per 500 km) | Comfort |
|---|---|---|
| Government (RSRTC, KSRTC, etc.) | ₹300-500 | Basic |
| Private non-AC | ₹500-800 | Moderate |
| Private AC sleeper | ₹700-1200 | Good |

- RedBus, IntrCity, and govt bus portals
- Overnight sleeper buses are a good option for 8-12 hour journeys
- Book 2-3 weeks in advance

### Flights

- Use student discount on Skyscanner/StudentUniverse
- Budget airlines: IndiGo, SpiceJet, Akasa Air
- Book 4-6 weeks in advance for lowest fares
- Travel with cabin luggage only to avoid baggage fees
- Flights within India can be ₹2000-4000 one-way if booked early

### Local transport at destination

- Metro — cheapest and fastest in cities that have it
- Bus — cheap but slow
- Auto-rickshaw / Rapido — use apps, not street hailing
- Share autos — cheapest option (found near metro stations and bus stands)
- Walk if distances are short and safe

## Accommodation

### Cheapest options

| Type | Cost/night | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dharamshala | ₹200-400 | Very basic, safe in religious cities |
| Hostel dorm | ₹500-800 | Zostel, Moustache, Hostel Mantra |
| PG shared room | ₹300-600 | For longer stays |
| Event-provided stay | Free | Many hackathons provide this |

### Splitting costs

- 2 people in a hostel dorm: ₹300-400 each
- 3-4 people in budget hotel (OYO): ₹200-400 each
- 4 people in Airbnb: ₹300-500 each

Always check if the event provides accommodation before booking yourself.

## Food

- Eat at student areas or university canteens (cheapest)
- Most hackathons provide meals — eat there
- Carry snacks and a water bottle
- Street food is cheap but check hygiene
- Avoid restaurants near tourist areas (overpriced)

## Packing checklist

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Reusable water bottle | Save money, stay hydrated |
| Portable charger | Essential for events |
| Power bank | For long days without charging |
| Extension cord | Outlets are always scarce at events |
| Earplugs / headphones | For sleeping in hostels or working in noisy areas |
| Small lock | For hostel lockers |
| Identification | Aadhaar, college ID, event confirmation |
| Emergency cash | ₹500-1000 in small notes |
| Basic first aid | Band-aids, ORS, paracetamol |

---

## Safety on a budget

- Travel in groups when possible
- Share your live location with someone at home
- Keep digital copies of all documents
- Stay at reputable hostels (read recent reviews)
- Do not carry all your money — use UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm)
- Keep emergency numbers saved

---

## General budgeting tips

- Use student discounts everywhere (carry your ID card)
- Use UPI — avoid ATM fees
- Cook if your accommodation has a kitchen
- Eat one big meal and two small meals (saves money)
- Use metro/bus instead of autos whenever possible
- Split everything with teammates/roommates
- Keep receipts if the event reimburses expenses
