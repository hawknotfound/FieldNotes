# Travel

Practical notes on travel for Indian students attending events in other cities.

## What this covers

- Planning travel for hackathons and competitions
- Budget travel tips
- What to pack
- Safety considerations
- City-specific logistics (see [cities/](../cities/index.md))

## Key principles

1. **Book early** — trains and buses fill up, especially for popular routes
2. **Travel overnight** — saves time and one night's accommodation
3. **Go in groups** — safer, cheaper, more fun
4. **Know the event location** — check distance from station/airport before booking
5. **Always have a backup plan** — for late trains, missed connections, etc.
