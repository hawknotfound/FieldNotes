# Travel

Practical notes on travel for Indian students attending events in other cities.

## What this covers

- Planning travel for hackathons and competitions
- Budget travel tips
- What to pack
- Safety considerations
- City-specific logistics (see [cities/](../cities/index.md))

## Key principles

1. **Book early** — trains and buses fill up, especially for popular routes
2. **Travel overnight** — saves time and one night's accommodation
3. **Go in groups** — safer, cheaper, more fun
4. **Know the event location** — check distance from station/airport before booking
5. **Always have a backup plan** — for late trains, missed connections, etc.

## Travel planning checklist

A week before any event trip, run through these:

### 1 week before
- [ ] Book confirmed tickets (train/bus/flight)
- [ ] Book accommodation
- [ ] Check if the event provides accommodation
- [ ] Calculate total budget (travel + stay + food + local transport + buffer)
- [ ] Inform parents/family about your plan
- [ ] Check if you need any permissions (college, parents)

### 2 days before
- [ ] Download offline maps of the destination city
- [ ] Save emergency contacts (event organizers, accommodation)
- [ ] Charge all devices and power bank
- [ ] Pack your bag (see packing list below)
- [ ] Confirm meetup plan with teammates if traveling together

### Day of travel
- [ ] Carry ID proof (Aadhaar, college ID, driving licence)
- [ ] Share live location with family/friends
- [ ] Confirm event registration and venue details
- [ ] Reach the station/airport 30+ minutes early

## City-specific logistics

When traveling to a new city for an event:
- Check the distance between the railway station/airport and the event venue
- Research which metro line or bus route serves the venue area
- Know which areas are safe for students and which to avoid
- Find student-friendly food options near the venue
- Check if the city has a dedicated guide in [cities/](../cities/index.md)

See [field-notes/lessons-from-travel.md](../field-notes/lessons-from-travel.md) for real travel experiences.

## Packing checklist for a hackathon/competition trip

### Essentials
- Laptop and charger (extension cord if in a team)
- Phone charger and power bank (10,000+ mAh)
- College ID and one additional ID proof
- Water bottle (reusable)
- Notebook and pen

### Comfort
- Light jacket or hoodie (venues are cold)
- Earphones/headphones and eye mask for sleeping
- Sleepers or comfortable shoes
- Small towel and toiletries (for multi-day events)
- Paracetamol, ORS packets, band-aids

### Optional but useful
- Mouse (if you prefer trackpad alternatives)
- Copy of event confirmation/ticket
- Printed resume (for networking)
- Visiting cards (if you have them)

## Safety notes for student travelers

- For solo travel: share location with someone you trust
- Use Ola/Uber or app-based autos for late-night movement, not street autos
- Keep emergency numbers on paper (not just on phone)
- Stay in well-reviewed hostels or known areas
- Do not leave your laptop or bag unattended at any point
- If traveling by general/sleeper class, keep valuables in a small bag you can hold
