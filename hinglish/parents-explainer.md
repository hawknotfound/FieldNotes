# Parents ko Kaise Samjhayein

Yeh guide un students ke liye hai jo apne parents ko samjhana chahte hain ki hackathons, competitions aur events mein jaana kyun important hai.

---

## Problem kya hai?

Indian parents generally:
- Studies ke alawa kuch aur time waste samajhte hain
- Travel ko unsafe maante hain
- Paisa kharch karne mein hesitant hote hain
- "Isse kya milega?" — yeh question poochte hain

## Solution kya hai?

Unke har concern ka specific answer do. General baatein nahi, concrete points.

---

### Concern 1: "Yeh sab studies se time waste hai"

**Tum kaho:**
- Hackathon mein jo coding seekhte hain, woh class mein nahi padhaya jaata
- Practical experience hota hai — companies ko yeh chahiye
- Smart India Hackathon government ka hai, Ministry of Education conduct karti hai
- Kuch colleges toh extra credit bhi dete hain participation ke liye
- Resume mein dalne se internships milne mein help hoti hai

### Concern 2: "Akele jaana safe nahi hai"

**Tum kaho:**
- Mere saath team ke 3-4 aur log hain
- Venue ek college campus hai, fully secured
- Main live location share karunga har time
- Organizers ka emergency number hai
- Pehle online event try kar ke dikhaunga ki safe hai

### Concern 3: "Paisa kharch hoga"

**Tum kaho:**
- Event free hai (most hackathons free hote hain)
- Kuch events mein khana bhi free milta hai
- Travel ka budget prepare kar ke dikhaunga
- Agar acha lage toh part-time freelance karke khud earn karunga
- Yehi investment hai — experience aur skill ke liye

### Concern 4: "Certificate ka kya karega?"

**Tum kaho:**
- Ye certificate job applications mein lagta hai
- College mein weightage milta hai kahi jagah
- Internship ke liye companies filter karti hain isi base pe
- Portfolio mein project dal sakta hoon jo mera skills dikhata hai

---

## Strategy

1. **Chhote step se shuru karo** — pehle online event, phir local city, phir national
2. **Success dikhao** — certificate, project, LinkedIn post — parents ko dikhao ki kuch seekha
3. **Teacher ko bolo bolne** — professor recommend kare toh parents maan jaate hain
4. **Budget bana ke do** — exact numbers ke saath plan do
5. **Calm raho** — gussa mat ho, facts ke saath baat karo

---

## Sample sentences

> "Maa/Papa, Smart India Hackathon Ministry of Education ka program hai. Isme government departments ke real problems solve karne hote hain. Mere college ke professor ne suggest kiya hai. Registration free hai. Main apni team ke saath participate karna chahta hoon. Kya aap website dekhna chahoge?"

> "Yeh competition resume mein dalne se mujhe internships milegi. Is saal bahut students ne iske through jobs paayi hain."

> "Main pehle online version try karunga. Agar sab acha raha toh agle saal offline jaunga."

---

## Yaad rakho

Parents darte hain risk se, unhe comfort do. Ek baar maan gaye, toh agli baar aasan ho jayega.
