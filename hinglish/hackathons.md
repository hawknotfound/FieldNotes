# Hackathons — Puri Jankari

Hackathon ek event hota hai jahan students milke koi problem solve karte hain. Generally 24-48 hours ka hota hai. Team banate ho, koi problem choose karte ho, aur uss problem ka solution (app, website, ya koi bhi tech product) bana ke submit kar dete ho.

## Hackathon kyun karna chahiye?

- **Real experience** — college mein theory padhate hain, hackathon mein actually kuch bana ke seekhte ho
- **Resume mein dikhta hai** — recruiters ko pasand aata hai
- **Prizes** — cash prizes milti hain (₹50,000 - ₹1,00,000+)
- **Network** — industry ke logo se milna hota hai
- **Certificates** — participation certificate bhi kaam aata hai
- **Confidence** — "main bhi kuch bana sakta hoon" ka feel aata hai

## Hackathon mein kya hota hai?

1. **Registration** karte ho pehle
2. **Team banate ho** (3-6 log, mix of skills)
3. **Problem statement choose karte ho**
4. **Solution banate ho** — coding, design, sab kuch
5. **Present karte ho** — judges ko pitch karte ho
6. **Winners announce hote hain**

## Pehli baar hackathon mein ja rahe ho?

- Koi complex idea mat lo. Simple leke aao jo kaam kare.
- Team mein roles fix karo — kaun code karega, kaun design, kaun pitch
- Mentors se help lo — woh isiliye hote hain
- Presentation practice karo — judges ko impress karna hai
- Sleep mat bhoolna — 24 hours straight kaam mushkil hota hai

## Common mistakes

- Bahut bada idea le liya jo time mein complete nahi hoga
- Team work nahi kiya — sab ek saath ek hi cheez pe kaam kar rahe
- Pitch practice nahi ki
- Last minute pe submit karne lage (technical issue hua toh fail)
