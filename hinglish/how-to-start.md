# Kaise Shuru Karein — Zero Se Hero

Yeh guide unke liye hai jo abhi kuch bhi nahi jaante, lekin shuru karna chahte hain.

---

## Step 0: Toh shuru kaise karein?

Pehle yeh decide karo ki tumhe kis field mein interest hai:

- **Coding / Tech** — hackathons, coding competitions, AI/ML
- **Business / Commerce** — startup competitions, pitch events, case studies
- **Design** — UI/UX, graphic design, product design
- **Research / Science** — olympiads, research internships
- **General** — management competitions, quizzing

Koi interest nahi hai? Don't worry. Coding try karo — scope sabse zyada hai.

---

## Step 1: Kuch basic skills lo

### Agar Coding mein jaana hai:

```
2 weeks — HTML, CSS basics (freeCodeCamp pe)
2 weeks — JavaScript basics
1 week — Git and GitHub
2 weeks — React ya Python (kisi ek mein command)
Phir — ek simple project banao
```

### Agar Business mein jaana hai:

```
Case studies padhna shuru karo
YouTube pe startup pitches dekho
Basic finance samjho (P&L, revenue, costs)
Excel seekh lo
```

### Agar Design mein jaana hai:

```
Figma download karo (free for students)
Basic design principles padho (youtube)
Kisi bhi app ka redesign karo practice ke liye
```

---

## Step 2: Pehla hackathon/competition dhundo

Beginner-friendly events:

- **Online hackathon** — Devfolio pe search karo "beginner friendly"
- **Unstop competitions** — filter by free, online
- **College fest** — apne ya neighbouring college ka fest dhundo
- **Coding contest** — CodeChef Starters (weekly, free, beginner level)

---

## Step 3: Team banao

- Tumhare college mein koi discord ya whatsapp group hoga — wahan dekh lo
- Agar team nahi milti, toh solo participate karo (online)
- Ya ek dost ko convince karo — saath seekhoge toh maza aayega

---

## Step 4: Participate karo (pehli baar)

- Goal: win nahi karna, bas participate karna
- Ek simple cheez banao jo kaam kare
- Complete karo chahe kuch bhi ho
- Experience notes likho — kya seekha, kya galat kiya

---

## Step 5: Repeat karo

- Har event ke baad thoda better banoge
- Portfolio mein projects add karte jao
- LinkedIn pe post karo
- Agli baar thoda complex idea lo

---

## Timeline for first 6 months

| Month | Goal |
|---|---|
| 1 | Basic skills sikho (coding ya koi bhi skill) |
| 2 | Online hackathon/competition mein participate karo |
| 3 | Ek small project banao |
| 4 | Local event mein jaao |
| 5 | National event ke liye register karo |
| 6 | Poore confidence ke saath participate karo |

---

## Zero se shuru karne walon ke liye tips

- YouTube pe har cheez free available hai — paisa mat lagao courses pe
- Community join karo (discord, telegram) — logo se seekho
- Doston ko bhi saath le lo — accountability milegi
- Kisi se compare mat karo — everyone starts somewhere
- Padhte raho, participate karte raho — 6 mahine mein bohot change aayega
