# Hinglish FieldNotes

Abe yaar, FieldNotes ka Hinglish section hai yeh.

Jo English folder mein detailed notes hain, wohi baatein yahan Hinglish mein likhi hain. Takki dostonn ke saath share karna easy ho, aur jo log comfortable English mein nahi hain woh bhi seekh sakein.

## Yeh folder kyun hai?

- Friends ke saath share karna easy ho
- Padhne mein natural lage
- Indian students ke liye relatable ho
- English mein jo samajh nahi aata, woh Hinglish mein clear ho

## Files in this folder

- [hackathons.md](hackathons.md) — hackathons kya hain, kaise participate karein
- [competitions.md](competitions.md) — competitions aur olympiads ki puri jankari
- [parents-explainer.md](parents-explainer.md) — parents ko kaise samjhayein
- [how-to-start.md](how-to-start.md) — shuruaat kaise karein (zero se hero)
- [networking.md](networking.md) — logo se kaise connect karein

## Important baat

Yeh mechanical translation nahi hai. Jo English folder mein hai, wohi baat yahan naturally Hinglish mein likhi gayi hai. Agar koi concept clear nahi ho, toh English folder check karo.

## Warning

Hinglish typing mein thoda casual tone hai. Lekin content utna hi useful hai jitna English ka.
