# Networking — Logo Se Kaise Connect Karein

Networking ka matlab sirf "LinkedIn pe connect karna" nahi hai. Asli networking hai — logo se milna, baat karna, help karna, aur relationships build karna jo baad mein kaam aaye.

---

## Networking kyun important hai?

- **Opportunities** — bahut saari jobs/internships public nahi hoti, reference se milti hain
- **Learning** — experienced logo se seekhne ko milta hai
- **Mentorship** — koi aapko guide kar sakta hai
- **Collaboration** — projects ke liye partners milte hain
- **Confidence** — logo se baat karne ki aadat lagti hai

---

## Online networking

### LinkedIn pe profile banao

Essentials:
- Professional photo
- Clean headline (like "Student at X | Building Y | Interested in Z")
- About section — 3-4 lines about what you do
- Projects and achievements list karo

### LinkedIn pe kaise post karein

- Post karo tumne kya seekha (learning in public)
- Post karo tumne kya banaya (project demos)
- Post karo event experiences
- Logo ke posts pe meaningful comment karo

**Mat karo:** "Please connect" bhejke chup ho jaana. Batao ki kyun connect kar rahe ho.

### Twitter (X) pe kaise use karein

- Tech Twitter India bohot active hai
- Follow builders, not just influencers
- Engage in conversations
- Share your learning journey

---

## Offline networking (events mein)

### Event se pehle

- Socho ki tum kis type ke logo se milna chahte ho
- Business cards ya LinkedIn ready rakho
- Comfortable shoes pehno (bahut khade rehna padta hai)

### Event ke time

- Introduction do: "Hi, I'm [name]. I'm working on [project/interest]."
- Questions poocho: "What brought you here?" "What are you working on?"
- Listen more, talk less
- Apna kaam genuine interest ke saath share karo
- Dono taraf se value do — "How can I help you?" puuchho

### Event ke baad

- 24-48 hours mein LinkedIn pe follow up karo
- Kuch specific mention karo jo baat hui thi
- Random connect request mat bhejdo — message likho

## Networking tips for introverts

- Quality over quantity — 10 genuine connections > 100 random connects
- Events mein ek ya do logo se deep baat karo
- Online pe comment/post se shuru karo
- Follow up karo — follow up hi actual networking hai
- Confidence fake karo initially, eventually real ho jayega

---

## Yaad rakhne wali baat

Networking ka matlab "logon ko use karna" nahi hai. Kaam karo, logo ki help karo, genuine raho. Baaki sab apne aap ho jayega.
