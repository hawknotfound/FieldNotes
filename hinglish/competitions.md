# Competitions aur Olympiads — Puri Jankari

India mein bahut saare competitions hote hain — school level se lekar national level tak. Yeh ek guide hai jo sabko samjhaega.

## Olympiads do prakar ke hote hain

### 1. National Olympiads (HBCSE wala path)

Yeh woh olympiads hain jo international level tak jaate hain. IIT, IISc, HBCSE jaise institutions conduct karte hain.

| Subject | Exam | International |
|---|---|---|
| Physics | NSEP → INPhO | IPhO |
| Chemistry | NSEC → INChO | IChO |
| Biology | NSEB → INBO | IBO |
| Maths | IOQM → RMO → INMO | IMO |
| Computer | ZIO/ZCO → INOI | IOI |

**Process:**
- Pehle NSE (National Standard Exam) — school mein hi hota hai, MCQ type
- Phir INO — theory + practical exam
- Phir OCSC — 2-3 week ka camp HBCSE Mumbai mein
- Top 4-6 students jaate hain international olympiad

**Kyun karein?**
- Deep knowledge aati hai
- IIT/NIT mein admission mein advantage
- Scholarships milti hain
- International travel ka experience

### 2. School Olympiads (SOF wala path)

SOF (Science Olympiad Foundation) har school ke liye olympiads conduct karta hai.

| Olympiad | Subject |
|---|---|
| NSO | Science |
| IMO | Maths |
| IEO | English |
| NCO | Computers |
| ICO | Commerce |

**Process:**
- School ke through registration
- Level 1 — school mein hi exam
- Level 2 — national level
- Classes 1 se 12 tak koi bhi de sakta hai

## Coding Competitions

Coding ke competitions bhi bohot hote hain:

| Platform | Best For |
|---|---|
| Codeforces | Global competitive programming |
| CodeChef | Indian CP scene, hirings |
| AtCoder | Beginner friendly contests |
| LeetCode | Interview preparation |

ICPC (International Collegiate Programming Contest) sabse bada coding competition hai. Team of 3, college students ke liye. IIT walon ka domination hai, lekin koi bhi participate kar sakta hai.

---

## Commerce Competitions

Commerce stream walon ke liye bhi bahut scope hai:

- **ICAI Commerce Olympiad** — ICAI conduct karta hai
- **ICSI Commerce Olympiad** — Company Secretaries ka exam
- **SOF ICO** — Commerce Olympiad
- **Hult Prize** — $1M ka prize, global startup competition
- **Pitch competitions** — IIMs ke fests mein business plan competitions

---

## Kaise shuru karein?

1. **School level (classes 6-10):** SOF olympiads, NTSE scholarship exam
2. **College prep (classes 11-12):** HBCSE olympiads (science), INOI (computing), IOQM (maths)
3. **College (UG):** ICPC, hackathons, startup competitions, GSOC
