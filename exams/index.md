# Exams

Notes on exams relevant to Indian students, with a focus on olympiads, scholarship exams, and competitions rather than standard board or entrance exams.

## What this covers

- Olympiad pathways (HBCSE, SOF, computing olympiads)
- Scholarship and talent exams
- Subject-specific competitive exams
- Exam strategies and resources

## Why olympiads and competitive exams matter

- They test deeper understanding than school exams
- Results are used for scholarships, research programs, and recognition
- Olympiad training builds problem-solving skills useful in all careers
- International olympiad participation is a significant achievement

## Preparation strategy for olympiads

Olympiads test depth, not breadth. Standard exam prep (memorize, practice past papers, repeat) helps less here.

1. **Build concepts first**: Before solving problems, ensure you understand the underlying theory. NCERT + standard reference books (HC Verma for physics, Irodov for advanced, Morrison & Boyd for chemistry).
2. **Solve for understanding, not speed**: In olympiads, accuracy matters more than speed. Spend time on each problem. Understand why the answer is what it is.
3. **Use past papers as learning tools**: Do not just time yourself. After solving, analyze: what concept was tested? What trick did they use? What alternative approaches exist?
4. **Join a problem-solving community**: Physics Olympiad groups, MathStackExchange, AoPS (Art of Problem Solving) forums. Discussing solutions deepens understanding.
5. **Teach others**: Explaining a solution to someone else is the best way to confirm you actually understand it.

## How to choose which exam to pursue

- For science students (classes 8-12): HBCSE olympiad pathway is the gold standard
- For math-focused students: IOQM → RMO → INMO → IMO Training Camp
- For computing: ZIO → ZCO → INOI → IOI Training Camp
- For scholarship + exposure: NTSE (class 10), KVPY (class 11-12), INSPIRE
- For commerce students: ICAI Commerce Olympiad, ICSI, business quizzes
- If you want recognition with less intensity: SOF olympiads, subject-specific contests

Start with one pathway. Master it before adding another. Olympiad success is about depth, not breadth.

## Common mistakes beginners make

- Starting serious prep only 1-2 months before the exam (most need 6-12 months)
- Ignoring theory and jumping straight to problem-solving
- Solving easy problems repeatedly instead of attempting harder ones
- Not analyzing mistakes after solving
- Competing alone without discussing with peers
- Comparing progress with top performers and getting discouraged
