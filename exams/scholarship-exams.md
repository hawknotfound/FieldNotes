# Scholarship Exams

Exams that lead to scholarships (financial support) for Indian students.

---

## NTSE (National Talent Search Examination)

- **Conducted by:** NCERT
- **For:** Class 10 students
- **Scholarship:** ₹1,250/month (for Class 11 to PhD)
- **Format:** Stage 1 (state-level), Stage 2 (national-level)
- **Subjects:** Mental Ability Test (MAT) + Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT)
- **Website:** [ncert.nic.in](https://ncert.nic.in)

NTSE is one of the most well-known scholarship exams in India. It provides financial support for the entire academic journey.

---

## INSPIRE Award (DST)

- **Conducted by:** Department of Science and Technology
- **For:** Class 6-10 students
- **Scholarship:** ₹5,000 for a project
- **Focus:** Innovation projects in science
- **Website:** [online-inspire.gov.in](https://online-inspire.gov.in)

INSPIRE Award is for school students to create innovative science projects. Selected projects receive ₹5,000 and may advance to higher levels.

---

## National Means-Cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMS)

- **Conducted by:** State governments (Centrally Sponsored Scheme)
- **For:** Class 8 students
- **Scholarship:** ₹12,000/year (₹1,000/month)
- **Eligibility:** Family income < ₹3.5 lakh/year
- **Format:** State-level exam

## Pre-Matric and Post-Matric Scholarships

Various government scholarships at the school level:

| Scholarship | For | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-Matric (Class 1-10) | SC/ST/OBC/Minority students | Ministry of Minority Affairs / State Govts |
| Post-Matric (Class 11 onwards) | SC/ST/OBC/EBC students | Ministry of Social Justice / State Govts |
| OBC Scholarship | OBC students (income < ₹1-2.5 lakh) | National Scholarship Portal |

## Olympiad-linked Scholarships

Some olympiad achievements lead to scholarships:
- **KVPY/INSPIRE** — for students who excel in science olympiads
- **IOI/INOI top performers** — sometimes receive scholarships from CodeChef, IARCS partners
- **IMO medalists** — recognized for scholarships by universities
- **HBCSE program participants** — training camp exposure leads to research opportunities

## How to Find More

1. **National Scholarship Portal** — [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)
2. **Buddy4Study** — [buddy4study.com](https://buddy4study.com)
3. **State scholarship portals** — each state has its own
4. **College scholarship offices** — many colleges have internal scholarships
5. **Corporate scholarships** — Infosys, TCS, Reliance, and others have programs

---

## Tips for scholarship exams

- Start preparing 6 months in advance
- Understand the specific eligibility criteria (community, income, marks)
- Keep documents ready (income certificate, caste certificate, mark sheets)
- Apply to multiple scholarships even if amounts are small
- Never pay anyone who promises to help you get a scholarship (most are scams)
- Track deadlines on a calendar — most have short application windows
