# Olympiad Pathways

Detailed breakdown of the major olympiad tracks in India.

---

## Science Olympiads (HBCSE Pathway)

### Subjects and tracks

| Subject | Exam Track | International Olympiad |
|---|---|---|
| Physics | NSEP → INPhO → OCSC (Ph) → | IPhO |
| Chemistry | NSEC → INChO → OCSC (Ch) → | IChO |
| Biology | NSEB → INBO → OCSC (Bio) → | IBO |
| Astronomy | NSEA → INAO → OCSC (Ast) → | IOAA |
| Junior Science | NSEJS → INJSO → OCSC (JS) → | IJSO |

### Stage 1: NSE (National Standard Exams)

- Conducted by IAPT (Indian Association of Physics Teachers)
- Usually held in November-December
- Multiple choice questions
- Syllabus: NCERT-based but deeper
- Registration through schools

### Stage 2: INO (Indian National Olympiad)

- Conducted by HBCSE, TIFR Mumbai
- Usually held in January-February
- Theory + experiment (for science subjects)
- Top performers qualify for OCSC

### Stage 3: OCSC (Orientation Cum Selection Camp)

- Held at HBCSE, Mumbai
- 2-3 week residential camp
- Training + selection exams
- Top 4-6 students selected for Indian team

### Stage 4: International Olympiad

- Held in different countries each year
- Indian team of 4-6 students + mentors
- Government-funded travel and participation

## Mathematics Olympiad Pathway

| Stage | Exam | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | IOQM (Indian Olympiad Qualifier in Mathematics) | September |
| 2 | RMO (Regional Mathematics Olympiad) | November |
| 3 | INMO (Indian National Mathematical Olympiad) | January |
| 4 | IMOTC (Training Camp) | April-May |
| 5 | IMO (International) | July |

- **Conducting body:** Mathematics Teachers' Association of India (MTAI)
- **IOQM registration:** Through mtai.org.in
- **Syllabus:** Algebra, number theory, combinatorics, geometry
- **Preparation:** Solve previous year problems, study from AoPS

---

## Informatics (Computing) Olympiad Pathway

| Stage | Exam | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ZIO (Zonal Informatics Olympiad) OR ZCO (Zonal Computing Olympiad) | November / February |
| 2 | INOI (Indian National Olympiad in Informatics) | February |
| 3 | IOITC (Training Camp) | April-May |
| 4 | IOI (International) | August |

- **Conducting body:** IARCS (Indian Association for Research in Computing Science)
- **Website:** [iarcs.org.in/inoi](https://iarcs.org.in/inoi)
- **Language:** C++ (primarily), Python, Java
- **Preparation:** Algorithms, data structures, practice from USACO and Codeforces

### Additional: EGOI (European Girls' Olympiad in Informatics)
- India participates since recent years
- India won its first gold medal at EGOI 2026
- Selection from INOI participants

## Preparation Resources

### Science Olympiads
- NCERT textbooks (thoroughly)
- Previous year NSE/INO papers (available on HBCSE website)
- IAPT website for syllabi and sample papers
- University-level textbooks (Resnick Halliday, Morrison & Boyd, Campbell Biology)

### Mathematics Olympiad
- Art of Problem Solving (AoPS) books and forums
- INMO previous year papers
- Problem Solving Strategies (Arthur Engel)
- Euclidean Geometry in Mathematical Olympiads (Evan Chen)

### Informatics Olympiad
- CP-Algorithms
- USACO Guide
- Codeforces problems (rated 1200-2000)
- Competitive Programming book (Halim brothers)
