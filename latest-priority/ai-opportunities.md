## AI Learning Path (Commerce × AI)

```
Month 1-2:   Google Cloud Skills Boost (AI/ML fundamentals)
Month 3-4:   Build With AI bootcamp (if available) OR Fast.ai
Month 5:     FutureX AI Challenge (project submission)
Month 6-8:   INAIO preparation (AI concepts + basic coding)
Month 9-12:  Open source AI project documentation contributions
```

## How to build an AI portfolio without coding much

1. **Write about AI** — Blog posts, LinkedIn articles explaining AI concepts in simple terms
2. **Document AI projects** — Contribute READMEs, tutorials, and user guides to open source AI tools
3. **Create AI use-case decks** — Industry analysis of how AI can transform banking, retail, finance
4. **Participate in AI ideathons** — Pitch AI solutions without writing code
5. **Learn AI ethics & policy** — Commerce + law + AI is a growing career niche

## AI skills most valuable for commerce students

| Skill | Why | How to learn |
|---|---|---|
| AI for Business Strategy | Apply AI to business problems | Case studies, business school resources |
| AI Prompt Engineering | Use AI tools effectively | Free courses (Google, DeepLearning.AI) |
| Data Interpretation | Read and question AI outputs | Statistics in Class 11-12 syllabus |
| AI Ethics & Policy | Regulatory/compliance angle | Free: MIT, Harvard online courses |
| No-code AI Tools | Build AI apps without coding | Bubble, Adalo, Google AI Studio |
