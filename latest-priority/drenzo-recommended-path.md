# Drenzo-Recommended 12-Month Path

A specific, step-by-step plan for a Class 11 commerce student (moving to Class 12) with AI interest. This assumes you're starting from scratch — no competition experience, beginner-level coding curiosity.

---

## Your Profile Assumptions

- **Class**: 11 (moving to 12 over this 12-month plan)
- **Stream**: Commerce
- **Coding**: Beginner (or zero)
- **AI interest**: High
- **Research/writing**: Strong
- **Available time**: ~5-7 hours/week outside school

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-2)

### Month 1: Orientation

**Goal**: Understand the landscape, set up systems

| Week | Action | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read `opportunities/live/index.md` and `latest-priority/top-10.md` fully | 2 hr |
| 2 | Set up Google Cloud Skills Boost account, take 1 free AI/ML lab | 3 hr |
| 3 | Read `decision-frameworks/opportunity-evaluation-framework.md` — apply to 3 opportunities | 2 hr |
| 4 | Create a Notion / Google Sheets tracker for all opportunities you want to attempt | 1 hr |

**Deliverable**: Personal opportunity tracker with 5-8 shortlisted items

### Month 2: Skill Building

**Goal**: Build minimum viable skills in AI and commerce

| Week | Action | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Complete 1 Google Cloud AI lab (no-code if possible) | 3 hr |
| 2 | Read `ai/ai-tools.md` — try 3 AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) | 2 hr |
| 3 | Start Fast.ai course (Lesson 1 — no coding background needed) | 4 hr |
| 4 | Write a 500-word LinkedIn post about what you learned | 1 hr |

**Deliverable**: 3 AI tools tested, 1 course started, 1 public post

## Phase 2: First Wins (Months 3-4)

**Goal**: Get your first competition entry done

| Week | Action | Time |

## Phase 3: Building Momentum (Months 5-7)

### Month 5: Hackathon Exposure

**Goal**: Join a hackathon team (as non-coder)

| Week | Action | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read `hackathons-worth-tracking.md` and pick 1 upcoming hackathon | 1 hr |
| 2 | Find teammates (school CS club, Unstop, LinkedIn) | 3 hr |
| 3 | Define roles — own documentation, business model, pitch | 2 hr |
| 4 | Start contributing to the project | 3 hr |

### Month 6: Deepen AI Knowledge

**Goal**: Move from beginner to intermediate AI understanding

| Week | Action | Time |
|---|---|---|

|---|---|---|

| 3-4 | Build your pitch deck and submit | 6 hr |

## What success looks like at the end of 12 months

### Great outcome
- 1 olympiad participation (ICAI or INAIO)
- 2 competition submissions (FutureX, National Ideathon)
- 1 hackathon team experience
- 1 open source contribution
- 1 AI case study / project
- Live portfolio with all of the above

### Amazing outcome (everything above +)
- Prize in one competition
- Strong open source track record (3+ contributions)
- GSoC acceptance (this is ambitious — requires heavy coding prep)
- Published essay or blog post on AI + commerce

### Keep going outcome
- 1 competition done (any)
- Clearer idea of what you want to do in college
- Comfort with AI tools
- Network of 5+ people in the space

## Common mistakes to avoid

| Mistake | Why it hurts | Better approach |
|---|---|---|
| Trying to do everything | Burnout, zero depth | Pick 3-5 events max |
| Waiting to be "ready" | Never start | Register first, learn along the way |
| Ignoring board exams | Academic damage | Plan around exam months |
| Solo on everything | Miss team skills | Join 1 team event |
| No documentation of work | Nothing to show | Write/record everything you build |
| Comparing with engineers | Discouragement | You bring commerce + AI — different value |
