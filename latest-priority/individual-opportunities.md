# Individual (Solo) Opportunities

You don't need a team. You don't need a college SPOC. You just need to show up and do the work.

These are ranked by how well they suit a Class 11–12 commerce student with AI/research interest.

## Strategy for solo participation

1. **Start with 1-2 essay competitions** — lowest effort, highest alignment with your skills
2. **Add 1 olympiad** — ICAI or SRCC NCO tests what you already study
3. **Try FutureX or National Ideathon** — project-based, no coding pressure
4. **If coding is going well, add INAIO** — the hardest but most impressive
5. **Plan GSoC 2027 NOW** — start making open source contributions today
