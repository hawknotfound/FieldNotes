# Olympiads Worth Considering

Not the math olympiad track (PRMO → RMO → INMO). That's for math prodigies. Here are olympiads that actually match a commerce + AI student's strengths.

---

## Commerce Olympiads

### 1. ICAI Commerce Olympiad

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Organizer** | ICAI |
| **Website** | [icaicommerceolympiad.com](https://www.icaicommerceolympiad.com) |
| **Schedule** | January (annual) |
| **Eligibility** | Class 8 to Graduation |
| **Format** | Online, 1 hour |
| **Cost** | Free |
| **Subjects** | Commerce, Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Aptitude |
| **Why take it** | Official ICAI olympiad. Free. Tests your syllabus. Career exposure to CA. |

## Comparison table

| Olympiad | Subject | Cost | Class | Solo? | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ICAI Commerce Olympiad | Commerce | Free | 8–Grad | Yes | Commerce students |
| SRCC NCO | Commerce | Free | 11–Grad | Yes | Top commerce aspirants |
| ICSI Commerce Olympiad | Commerce | Free | School | Yes | CS career curiosity |
| SilverZone ISCO | Commerce | Nominal | 2–12 | Yes | Olympiad collectors |
| INAIO | AI | Free | 8–12 | Yes | AI career aspirants |
| SOF NCO | Cyber/CS | Nominal | 1–12 | Yes | Tech foundation |
| SOF IEO | English | Nominal | 1–12 | Yes | Writing skill proof |
| SOF IGKO | GK | Nominal | 1–12 | Yes | Current affairs habit |

## Recommendation

- **Must do**: ICAI Commerce Olympiad + INAIO (covers both your streams)
- **Good to add**: SRCC NCO (for SRCC aspirants)
- **If you have time**: SilverZone ISCO or one SOF olympiad
