# Commerce × AI Guide — Hinglish Version

Doston ke saath share karne ke liye. No formal English. Real talk.

---

## Yeh guide kiske liye hai?

Agar tu Class 11 ya 12 ka commerce student hai, coding thodi bahut seekh raha hai (ya seekhna chahta hai), AI mein interest hai, aur kuch **real** karna chahta hai — padhai ke alawa — toh yeh teri hai.

---

## Top 5 cheezein jo abhi kar sakta hai

### 1. ICAI Commerce Olympiad — FREE, online, 1 ghanta

CA body ka official olympiad. Accounts, business, economics — jo tu padh raha hai wahi aata hai.

- **Website**: icaicommerceolympiad.com
- **Fee**: Free
- **Time**: 1 hour online
- **Kab hota hai**: January (har saal)

> Seedha syllabus se connected hai. 1 hafte ki taiyari kaam aayegi.

### 2. Indian National AI Olympiad (INAIO) — India ka唯一 AI olympiad

ACM India conduct karta hai. School students ke liye. International AI Olympiad ka selection isi se hota hai.

- **Website**: india.acm.org/education/inaio
- **Fee**: Free
- **Coding chahiye?**: Haan, but basic level pe seekh sakta hai

> Agar AI mein career soch raha hai, yeh must-do hai.

### 3. FutureX AI Challenge — Bina code ke bhi participate kar sakta hai

AI project submit karna hota hai. Non-coding tracks bhi hain — research, design, presentation.

- **Website**: thewonderminds.com/futureX.html
- **Fee**: Free
- **Coding?**: Optional

> Perfect for commerce students — AI + research dono dikha sakta hai.

### 4. Blue Ocean Entrepreneurship Competition — Global stage

5-minute pitch video bhejni hoti hai apni business idea ki. No coding. Global competition.

- **Website**: blueoceancompetition.org
- **Fee**: Free
- **Prize**: $500-$1000 cash

> Commerce student ke liye natural fit. Entrepreneurship + competition.

### 5. School Youth Ideathon (SYI) — CBSE recognised

School level ka entrepreneurship competition. Idea submit karna hota hai.

- **Website**: youthideathon.in
- **Fee**: Free (school register karti hai)
- **Kab**: August-October mein registration

> CBSE backing. Principal se baat kar ke school ko register karwa.

## Bina code ke hackathon mein kaam kaise karein?

Engineers wale hackathons mein bhi non-coders ki zaroorat hoti hai. Tu yeh kar sakta hai:

1. **Business model** — Product kaise paise kamaega
2. **Research** — Market size, competitors
3. **Pitch deck** — Presentation (most teams win on pitch, not code)
4. **UI/UX** — Figma se wireframes banao (no coding)
5. **Documentation** — README, project report
6. **Demo script** — Judges ko kya dikhana hai

> 1-2 coders dhundh, upar wali skills leke jaa. Team tujhe welcome karegi.
