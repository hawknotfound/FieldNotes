# First Opportunity Roadmap

A step-by-step plan for finding and applying to your first real opportunity outside the classroom — a hackathon, fellowship, competition, or program.

## Overview

This is different from the hackathon roadmap. That assumes you have found an event and are preparing for it. This helps you find your first opportunity in the first place.

**Timeframe**: 2-4 weeks from start to submission

## Phase 1: Discover (Week 1)

### Step 1: Define your starting point
Answer three questions in a note:
1. What am I interested in? (coding, design, writing, business, science, something else)
2. What skill level am I at? (beginner, intermediate, advanced)
3. How much time can I commit? (weekend, few hours/week, full-time)

### Step 2: Find opportunities that match
Check these sources:
- [Unstop](https://unstop.com) — largest platform for Indian student competitions
- [Devfolio](https://devfolio.co) — hackathons across India
- [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com) — search "hackathon India" or "fellowship India"
- College notice boards and WhatsApp groups
- Existing folder: [opportunities/](../opportunities/index.md) for fellowships and scholarships

### Step 3: Shortlist 3 opportunities
Pick ones where:
- You meet most eligibility criteria
- The deadline is at least 7 days away
- The time commitment fits your schedule
- The topic genuinely interests you (not just because it looks good)

## Phase 2: Prepare (Week 2)

### Step 4: Research each opportunity thoroughly
For each shortlisted opportunity:
- Read the full rules and guidelines
- Look at past winners or participants (LinkedIn, event website)
- Understand what the selection criteria are
- Find out what format submissions need to be in

### Step 5: Gather what you need
- Update your basic profile (LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio)
- Prepare a brief introduction about yourself (30 seconds)
- If a team is needed, find teammates (friends, college groups, Discord servers)
- Gather any documents needed (ID, resume, transcripts)

### Step 6: Start your submission early
Do not wait until the last week. Start now.
- For hackathons: brainstorm ideas, learn any new technology needed
- For fellowships: start the application essays early
- For competitions: begin working on the project or preparation

## Phase 3: Execute (Week 3-4)

### Step 7: Finish and submit
- Set a personal deadline 48 hours before the official deadline
- Get someone else to review your submission
- Make sure all required fields are filled
- Confirm submission received (confirmation email or screenshot)

### Step 8: Regardless of outcome, document
Write down:
- What you learned from the process
- What you would do differently
- What other opportunities you found during this search

## What if nothing works out?

If none of your shortlisted opportunities work out:
- Look for smaller, less competitive events first
- Join a community where opportunities are shared (see [networking/communities.md](../networking/communities.md))
- Build a small project or write about a topic you care about — this creates its own opportunities
- Try again next month. Opportunities are recurring. Missing one is not missing your chance.

## Checklist

- [ ] Defined my interests and commitment level
- [ ] Searched 4+ platforms for opportunities
- [ ] Shortlisted 3 matching opportunities
- [ ] Read full guidelines for each
- [ ] Started submission or preparation
- [ ] Set an internal deadline before official deadline
- [ ] Submitted and confirmed receipt
- [ ] Documented the process for next time
