# Cities

Student-focused guides to major Indian cities for event participation, internships, and travel.

## Purpose

When you attend a hackathon, competition, or conference in a new city, knowing the practical details — cheap accommodation, student-friendly areas, local transport, food options — makes the experience smoother.

## What each city guide covers

- Why students visit this city (events, opportunities, culture)
- Student-friendly areas and neighborhoods
- Budget accommodation options
- Local transport (metro, buses, auto-rickshaws)
- Coworking spaces and libraries for remote work
- Food options for students
- Safety notes
- Connectivity (airport, railway, bus)
- Estimated daily budget

## How to use

1. Check if the city has a dedicated guide below
2. Read the guide for practical tips before booking travel
3. Cross-reference with the [travel folder](../travel/index.md) for broader tips
