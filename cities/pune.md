# Pune

A major student city with a strong education ecosystem and growing technology sector.

## Why Pune

- One of India's largest student populations
- COEP, VIT Pune, Symbiosis, and many other colleges
- Strong automotive and manufacturing presence
- Growing IT and startup scene (especially in Hinjawadi)
- Pune is more affordable than Mumbai and Bangalore
- Major cultural and tech fests

## Student-friendly areas

| Area | Why | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| Kothrud | Student hub, affordable, good food | Low-Mid |
| KP (Koregaon Park) | Cafes, events, startup scene | Mid |
| Viman Nagar | Near airport, modern | Mid |
| FC Road | Central, student crowd | Low-Mid |
| Hinjawadi | IT hub, near many corporate events | Mid |
| Shivajinagar | Near COEP, affordable | Low |

## Budget accommodation

- **Hostels:** Hostel Mantra (KP area) — ₹500-800/night
- **PGs:** ₹5,000-10,000/month in Kothrud, Shivajinagar
- **Budget hotels:** ₹600-1000/night near railway station or bus stand
- **Service apartments:** ₹700-1200/night (short stays)

## Transport

| Mode | Details |
|---|---|
| PMPML buses | Moderate coverage |
| Auto-rickshaw | Most common, use apps |
| Metro | Limited (PCMC-Swargate line operational) |
| Rapido | ₹15-50 short distances |
| Ola/Uber | ₹100-300 within city |

**Note:** Pune has good road connectivity. Autos are easier to find than app-based cabs.

## Food on a budget

- Misal pav (₹30-70) — Pune specialty
- South Indian meals ₹50-80
- College canteen food ₹30-60
- MG Road and FC Road have affordable eats
- University area restaurants are very cheap

## Connectivity

- **Airport:** Pune International Airport (at Viman Nagar)
- **Railway:** Pune Junction, Shivajinagar
- **Bus:** Pune station buses connect to most cities

## Safety

- One of the safer Indian cities for students
- Kothrud, KP, Viman Nagar are very safe
- Women generally find Pune comfortable
- Standard precautions apply at night

## Coworking & workspaces

- WeWork (KP, Viman Nagar) — ₹600-900/day
- GoWork (SB Road) — ₹300-500/day
- 91springboard (Kothrud) — ₹300-500/day
- Cafes in KP (Le Flamington, Starbucks) — ₹200-400 for a few hours
