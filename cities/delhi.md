# Delhi

The national capital is a major hub for government-linked events, large conferences, and college fests.

## Why Delhi

- Central location for national-level government events
- Headquarters of many ministries and government bodies
- IIT Delhi, DTU, NSUT, and other top engineering colleges with major fests
- Large startup ecosystem in Delhi-NCR (Gurugram, Noida)
- India International Trade Fair and other large expos
- Affordable compared to Mumbai

## Budget accommodation

- **Hostels:** Zostel Delhi (Hauz Khas), Moustache Hostel — ₹500-900/night
- **PGs:** ₹5,000-10,000/month in Dwarka, GTB Nagar, Mukherjee Nagar
- **Budget hotels:** ₹600-1200/night in Paharganj or Karol Bagh
- **Dharamshalas:** ₹200-500/night (basic but very cheap)

## Transport

| Mode | Details |
|---|---|
| Metro | Excellent coverage, connects most areas |
| DTC buses | Cheap but crowded |
| Auto-rickshaw | Use apps, ₹50-150 short distances |
| Rapido | ₹15-60 short distances |

**Note:** Delhi Metro is the best urban transport system in India. Get a tourist card for unlimited rides.

## Coworking & workspaces

- WeWork (Connaught Place, Aerocity) — ₹600-1000/day
- India Habitat Centre — ₹300-500/day (Lodhi Road)
- The Office Pass (multiple locations) — ₹300-600/day
- CoWrks (Cyber City, Gurugram) — ₹500-800/day

## Food on a budget

- Street food (chole bhature ₹30-50, momos ₹30-60)
- University canteens (accessible to visitors in most colleges)
- South Indian meals ₹50-80
- Dhaba meals in campus areas ₹60-120

## Connectivity

- **Airport:** Indira Gandhi International (IGI) — T1 and T3
- **Railway:** New Delhi, Old Delhi, Hazrat Nizamuddin, Anand Vihar
- **Bus:** ISBT Kashmere Gate, Anand Vihar, Sarai Kale Khan

## Safety

- Metro is safe even at night
- Some areas (Paharganj, Old Delhi) require caution
- Women should avoid isolated areas late at night
- Connaught Place and Hauz Khas are safe
