# Mumbai

India's financial and entertainment capital. Home to corporate headquarters, major conferences, and a growing startup ecosystem.

## Why Mumbai

- Corporate HQ of most Indian and multinational companies
- IIT Bombay, SP Jain, NMIMS — top college fests
- Active startup and venture capital ecosystem
- Major conferences: NASSCOM, TiE, TechSparks
- Gateway to corporate internships and jobs
- Strong media and entertainment industry events

## Student-friendly areas

| Area | Why | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| Andheri West | Near airport, affordable, many events | Mid |
| Vile Parle | Near IIT Bombay, student-friendly | Mid |
| Dadar | Central connectivity, affordable | Low-Mid |
| Powai | IIT Bombay area, green, safe | Mid-High |
| Malad/Kandivali | Far but affordable | Low |

## Budget accommodation

- **Hostels:** Zostel Mumbai (Juhu), Hostel@Mumbai — ₹600-1200/night
- **PGs:** ₹7,000-15,000/month in Andheri, Malad
- **Budget hotels:** ₹800-1500/night in Andheri, Dadar
- **Dharamshalas:** ₹300-500/night (very basic)

## Transport

| Mode | Details |
|---|---|
| Local train | Lifeline of Mumbai, very crowded |
| BEST buses | Covers most areas |
| Metro | Limited lines (Versova-Andheri-Ghatkopar, etc.) |
| Auto-rickshaw | Only in suburbs, use apps |
| Taxi | Kaali-Peeli (metered), app-based |

**Note:** Local trains are the fastest way to travel but extremely crowded during peak hours (8-11am, 5-9pm). Avoid peak times.

## Coworking & workspaces

- WeWork (BKC, Andheri, Lower Parel) — ₹700-1200/day
- Awfis (multiple locations) — ₹400-800/day
- CoWrks (BKC, Andheri) — ₹500-900/day
- 91springboard (Andheri, Vile Parle) — ₹350-600/day

## Food on a budget

- Vada pav (₹10-20) — Mumbai's staple
- South Indian meals ₹60-100
- Irani cafes (Bun maska, chai) ₹30-60
- College canteens around IIT Bombay, VJTI
- Street food: pani puri, pav bhaji (₹30-60)

## Connectivity

- **Airport:** Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International (T1 domestic, T2 international)
- **Railway:** CSMT (main), Dadar, Bandra, Andheri, Borivali
- **Bus:** Various ST depots (inter-state)

## Safety

- Generally safe, but crowded areas have pickpocket risk
- Local trains have Ladies compartments (recommended for women)
- Avoid isolated areas late at night
- Andheri, Bandra, Powai are relatively safe
