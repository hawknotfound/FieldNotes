# Hyderabad

A fast-growing tech hub with a strong student ecosystem, affordable cost of living, and major college fests.

## Why Hyderabad

- Growing startup and IT ecosystem (HITEC City, Gachibowli)
- IIIT Hyderabad, IIT Hyderabad, UoH — top research institutions
- Affordable city compared to Bangalore and Mumbai
- Active hackathon and conference scene
- T-Hub — one of India's largest innovation hubs
- Good weather year-round

## Student-friendly areas

| Area | Why | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| Gachibowli | Near IIIT, IIT, tech offices | Mid |
| HITEC City | Tech hub, corporate events | Mid |
| Madhapur | Startup hub, events, cafes | Mid |
| Kukatpally | Very affordable, student area | Low |
| Himayatnagar | Central, affordable | Low-Mid |
| Jubilee Hills | High-end but has events | High |

## Budget accommodation

- **Hostels:** Zostel Hyderabad (Madhapur) — ₹500-800/night
- **PGs:** ₹4,000-8,000/month in Kukatpally, Gachibowli
- **Budget hotels:** ₹600-1000/night near railway station or Abids
- **Service apartments:** ₹700-1200/night (short stays)

## Transport

| Mode | Details |
|---|---|
| Metro | Red/Blue/Green lines, covers major areas |
| TSRTC buses | Good coverage |
| Auto-rickshaw | Use apps, ₹50-150 short distances |
| Rapido | ₹15-50 short distances |
| Ola/Uber | ₹100-300 within city |

**Note:** Hyderabad Metro is the second-largest in India after Delhi. It connects key areas.

## Food on a budget

- Hyderabadi biryani (₹100-200) — a must-try
- South Indian meals ₹50-80
- Street food around universities ₹20-50
- Paradise and Bawarchi are famous biryani spots
- Mess food in student areas ₹40-70/meal

## Connectivity

- **Airport:** Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (RGIA) — 25 km from city
- **Railway:** Hyderabad Deccan (Nampally), Secunderabad, Kacheguda
- **Bus:** Mahatma Gandhi Bus Station (inter-state)

## Safety

- One of the safest major cities in India
- Women feel safe even late in tech areas (Gachibowli, Madhapur)
- Old City area requires more caution
- Standard safety practices apply

## Coworking & workspaces

- T-Hub (Gachibowli) — India's largest innovation hub
- WeWork (HITEC City) — ₹600-900/day
- 91springboard (Madhapur) — ₹300-500/day
- The Office (Jubilee Hills) — ₹400-600/day
