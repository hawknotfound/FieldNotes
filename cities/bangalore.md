# Bangalore (Bengaluru)

India's startup capital and the city with the most hackathons, tech conferences, and student events.

## Why Bangalore

- Highest density of tech events in India
- Headquarters of most major tech companies
- Active meetup scene (GDG, AWS User Groups, Python communities)
- Many college fests with hackathons
- Bengaluru Tech Summit (annual, November)
- Startup ecosystem: countless incubators, accelerators, investor events

## Student-friendly areas

| Area | Why | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| Koramangala | Most events, cafes, startup hub | Mid |
| Indiranagar | Great cafes, co-working, events | Mid-High |
| HSR Layout | Student-friendly, affordable | Low-Mid |
| JP Nagar | Quiet, affordable, good food | Low-Mid |
| BTM Layout | Very affordable, near tech parks | Low |
| MG Road | Central, metro access | Mid |

## Budget accommodation

- **Hostels:** Zostel (Koramangala, Indiranagar), Hostel Mantra — ₹500-1000/night
- **PGs:** Monthly stays ₹6,000-12,000 depending on area
- **Budget hotels:** ₹800-1500/night near HSR, BTM
- **Airbnb shared rooms:** ₹600-1200/night

## Transport

| Mode | Details |
|---|---|
| Metro | Green line (North-South), Purple line (East-West), expanding |
| BMTC buses | Extensive network, ₹10-50 per ride |
| Auto-rickshaw | Use apps (Ola/Uber/Rapido), ₹50-200 short distances |
| Rapido (bike taxi) | ₹15-50 short distances, fastest for traffic |

**Note:** Bangalore traffic is among the worst in India. Plan travel with 2x buffer time.

## Coworking & workspaces

- WeWork (multiple locations) — day passes ₹500-800
- BHIVE (Koramangala, Indiranagar) — ₹300-500/day
- 91springboard — ₹350-600/day
- Coffee shops in Koramangala (Third Wave Coffee, Matteo's)

## Food on a budget

- South Indian meals (₹50-100) — Udupi restaurants everywhere
- North Indian thalis in Koramangala (₹100-150)
- Street food (₹20-50)
- Mess food in student areas like HSR and BTM (₹40-80/meal)

## Connectivity

- **Airport:** Kempegowda International Airport (KIAL) — 35-40 km from city
- **Railway:** Yesvantpur, KSR (City), Cantonment
- **Bus:** Majestic Bus Station (inter-state and intra-city)

## Safety

- Generally safe compared to other Indian metros
- Avoid walking alone late at night in isolated areas
- Koramangala, Indiranagar, HSR are relatively safe
- Women should use app-based cabs at night
