# Commerce & Business Competitions

For students in commerce, business studies, economics, and related fields. These competitions test knowledge of accounting, finance, law, and business strategy.

---

## ICAI Commerce Olympiad

- **Conducted by:** Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI)
- **Eligibility:** Classes 8 to Graduation
- **Format:** Online, multiple-choice questions
- **Frequency:** Annual
- **Prizes:** Cash, certificates, recognition
- **Website:** [icai.org](https://icai.org)

Covers accounting, finance, economics, and business studies.

---

## ICSI Commerce Olympiad

- **Conducted by:** Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI)
- **Eligibility:** Classes 11-12 (Commerce stream)
- **Format:** Online/offline, 40 MCQs, 50 minutes
- **Prizes:** Up to ₹50,000 for international rank 1
- **Website:** [icsi.edu](https://icsi.edu)

Focuses on commerce, economics, accounting, business studies, and corporate governance.

---

## SOF International Commerce Olympiad (ICO)

- **Conducted by:** Science Olympiad Foundation
- **Eligibility:** Classes 1-12
- **Format:** School-level exam
- **Website:** [sofworld.org](https://sofworld.org)

Tests commercial awareness, business knowledge, and economics concepts.

---

## Hult Prize

- **Global student startup competition**
- **Prize:** $1,000,000 in seed funding
- **Theme-based** (SDG-aligned) each year
- **Indian participation:** Large, with campus-level rounds
- **Website:** [hultprize.org](https://hultprize.org)

Hult Prize is the world's largest student social entrepreneurship competition. Indian teams have won and been finalists multiple times.

---

## Pitch Karo India

- **Format:** Startup pitching competition
- **Prize pool:** ₹3,00,000
- **Open to:** Students and early-stage startups
- **Website:** pitchkaroindia.com

India-focused pitching competition with investor networking opportunities.

---

## Youth Co:Lab National Innovation Challenge

- **Organizers:** Atal Innovation Mission (NITI Aayog) + UNDP
- **Focus:** Social entrepreneurship, sustainable consumption
- **Eligibility:** Youth-led startups (founders aged 15-29)
- **Prizes:** Seed grants, mentorship, incubation
- **Website:** [aim.gov.in](https://aim.gov.in)

## College-level business competitions

Most major B-schools and colleges host business plan competitions:

| Institution | Competition | Prize (approx) |
|---|---|---|
| IIM Ahmedabad | Confluence B-Plan | ₹5-10 lakhs |
| IIM Bangalore | Vista B-Plan | ₹3-5 lakhs |
| IIM Calcutta | Intaglio B-Plan | ₹3-5 lakhs |
| SRCC | SRCC Business Conclave | ₹1-2 lakhs |
| St. Stephen's | Stephen's B-Plan | ₹1 lakh |

These are usually part of annual fests and are open to all college students.
