# Olympiads in India

India has two parallel olympiad ecosystems: the **HBCSE national olympiad pathway** (leading to international olympiads) and the **SOF/Silverzone school olympiads** (mass-participation exams).

---

## HBCSE National Olympiad Pathway

The Homi Bhabha Centre for Science Education (TIFR) conducts the Indian National Olympiad program in partnership with IAPT and other organizations. This is the pathway to represent India at international olympiads.

### Subjects available

| Olympiad | International Counterpart | Website |
|---|---|---|
| Physics (INPhO) | IPhO | olympiads.hbcse.tifr.res.in |
| Chemistry (INChO) | IChO | same |
| Biology (INBO) | IBO | same |
| Astronomy (INAO) | IOAA | same |
| Junior Science (INJSO) | IJSO | same |
| Mathematics (INMO) | IMO | mtai.org.in |
| Informatics (INOI) | IOI | iarcs.org.in/inoi |

### General selection stages

For science subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Astronomy):

1. **NSE (National Standard Exam)** — conducted by IAPT, multiple-choice, school-level
2. **INO (Indian National Olympiad)** — conducted by HBCSE, theory + experiment
3. **OCSC (Orientation Cum Selection Camp)** — at HBCSE, Mumbai, ~2-3 weeks
4. **International Olympiad** — Indian team selected from OCSC

For Mathematics:
- IOQM → RMO → INMO → IMOTC → IMO

For Informatics (Computing):
- ZIO/ZCO → INOI → IOITC → IOI

### Eligibility

- Typically classes 8-12 for science subjects
- Classes 9-12 for informatics
- Indian citizenship/residency required

### How to prepare

- Start with NCERT concepts, go deeper
- Solve previous year papers (available on HBCSE website)
- Join preparation communities like AoPS (Art of Problem Solving)
- Attend summer camps and training programs
- Use standard textbooks (University Physics, Morrison & Boyd, etc.)

## SOF School Olympiads

The Science Olympiad Foundation conducts mass-participation olympiads for classes 1-12.

### Exams offered

| Exam | Subject |
|---|---|
| NSO | National Science Olympiad |
| IMO | International Mathematics Olympiad |
| IEO | International English Olympiad |
| IGKO | International General Knowledge Olympiad |
| NCO | National Cyber Olympiad |
| ICO | International Commerce Olympiad |

### Format
- Two levels: Level 1 (school-level), Level 2 (national-level)
- Multiple-choice questions
- Exam held during school hours
- Results within 6-8 weeks

### Registration
- Through your school
- Schools register with SOF, students register through schools
- Website: [sofworld.org](https://sofworld.org)

## Commerce Olympiads

For students interested in commerce:

| Olympiad | Conducted by | Classes |
|---|---|---|
| ICAI Commerce Olympiad | ICAI | 8 to graduation |
| ICSI Commerce Olympiad | ICSI | 11-12 |
| SOF ICO | SOF | 1-12 |

These test knowledge of accounting, economics, business studies, and finance.
