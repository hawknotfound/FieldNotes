# Competitions

This folder covers student competitions in India — from olympiads and coding contests to business and commerce competitions.

## Categories covered

- **Olympiads** — science, maths, informatics, commerce olympiads
- **Coding competitions** — ICPC, CodeChef, Codeforces, and others
- **Commerce competitions** — ICAI, ICSI olympiads, business quizzes
- **Subject-specific contests** — physics, chemistry, biology, astronomy olympiads

## Why competitions matter

- Test your knowledge beyond the curriculum
- Build problem-solving skills and mental endurance
- Get national and international recognition
- Strengthen college applications and resumes
- Qualify for training camps and advanced programs
- Win prizes, scholarships, and mentorship

## Key conducting bodies in India

| Body | Conducts |
|---|---|
| HBCSE (TIFR) | Indian National Olympiads (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Astronomy) |
| IARCS | Indian Computing Olympiad (pathway to IOI) |
| MTAI | IOQM, RMO, INMO (Mathematics) |
| SOF | NSO, IMO, IEO, ICO, NCO (school level) |
| ICAI / ICSI | Commerce Olympiads |
| ACM India | India AI Olympiad |

## Notes

- Most olympiads are free or have nominal participation fees
- School-level olympiads (SOF) are accessible to students from class 1-12
- National olympiads (HBCSE pathway) start from class 8-12
- Coding competitions are open to college students with no upper age limit in most cases
