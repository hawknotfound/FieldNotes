# Competitions

This folder covers student competitions in India — from olympiads and coding contests to business and commerce competitions.

## Categories covered

- **Olympiads** — science, maths, informatics, commerce olympiads
- **Coding competitions** — ICPC, CodeChef, Codeforces, and others
- **Commerce competitions** — ICAI, ICSI olympiads, business quizzes
- **Subject-specific contests** — physics, chemistry, biology, astronomy olympiads

## Why competitions matter

- Test your knowledge beyond the curriculum
- Build problem-solving skills and mental endurance
- Get national and international recognition
- Strengthen college applications and resumes
- Qualify for training camps and advanced programs
- Win prizes, scholarships, and mentorship

## Key conducting bodies in India

| Body | Conducts |
|---|---|
| HBCSE (TIFR) | Indian National Olympiads (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Astronomy) |
| IARCS | Indian Computing Olympiad (pathway to IOI) |
| MTAI | IOQM, RMO, INMO (Mathematics) |
| SOF | NSO, IMO, IEO, ICO, NCO (school level) |
| ICAI / ICSI | Commerce Olympiads |
| ACM India | India AI Olympiad |

## How to choose which competition to pursue

| Your situation | Best competition type |
|---|---|
| School student (class 6-10), science interest | SOF olympiads → HBCSE pathway |
| School student (class 8-12), strong in science/math | HBCSE olympiads directly |
| School student, interest in computing | ZIO → ZCO → INOI |
| College student, coding focus | ICPC, CodeChef, Codeforces |
| College student, business/commerce focus | ICAI olympiad, case study competitions |
| Any student, general interest | Quizzes, essay competitions, subject olympiads |

## Preparation timeline for olympiads

### 12 months before exam
- Choose your subject
- Gather standard reference books
- Build conceptual foundation (NCERT + reference books)
- Join online forums (AoPS, Reddit, Discord)

### 6 months before exam
- Start solving past papers (1 per week initially, then 2 per week)
- Identify weak areas and revisit theory
- Discuss solutions with peers or mentors
- Time yourself occasionally

### 3 months before exam
- Solve past papers under timed conditions
- Focus on speed + accuracy
- Target specific score improvements

### 1 month before exam
- Light revision of all topics
- Solve 1-2 full papers per week
- Rest properly before exam day

## Common mistakes in competition prep

- **Underestimating theory**: Olympiads test deep understanding, not just problem-solving tricks. Theory is essential.
- **Solving easy problems repeatedly**: You need to struggle with hard problems to improve. Comfort is a trap.
- **Ignoring one weak topic**: A single weak area can cost you selection. Address it early.
- **No peer discussion**: Solving alone limits growth. Discussing with others exposes you to different approaches.
- **Giving up after one bad attempt**: Most olympiad participants do not qualify on their first try. Multiple attempts are normal.

## Notes

- Most olympiads are free or have nominal participation fees
- School-level olympiads (SOF) are accessible to students from class 1-12
- National olympiads (HBCSE pathway) start from class 8-12
- Coding competitions are open to college students with no upper age limit in most cases
