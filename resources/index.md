# Resources

Curated list of platforms, tools, and communities useful for Indian students.

## What this covers

- Platforms to find hackathons, competitions, and opportunities
- Learning resources
- Productivity tools
- Communities and networks
- Government portals

## Why a curated list

There are many platforms, but finding the right ones saves time. This list focuses on resources that are actively used by Indian students and have proven value.

## Files in this folder

- [platforms-communities.md](platforms-communities.md) — detailed breakdown of key platforms
