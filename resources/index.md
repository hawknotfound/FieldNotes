Curated list of platforms, tools, and communities useful for Indian students.

- Learning resources

## Why a curated list

There are many platforms, but finding the right ones saves time. This list focuses on resources that are actively used by Indian students and have proven value.

## Files in this folder

- [platforms-communities.md](platforms-communities.md) — detailed breakdown of key platforms

## How to choose the right resource

For any skill or topic, you will find dozens of resources. The right one depends on:
- **Your learning style**: Do you prefer reading, watching, or building?
- **Your current level**: Beginner resources frustrate advanced learners. Advanced resources overwhelm beginners.
- **Your goal**: Learning for a hackathon (fast, practical) is different from learning for an exam (depth, theory).
- **Your time**: A 40-hour course is not better than a 2-hour guide if you only have 2 hours.

General rule: start with the official documentation or a well-rated beginner resource. Switch if it does not click within 2 sessions.

## Evaluating a resource before committing

1. **Check the date**: A tutorial from 2019 may use outdated libraries or frameworks. Check the publication/update date.
2. **Check the audience**: Is it aimed at beginners or advanced users? Look at the first lesson to gauge difficulty.
3. **Check reviews**: YouTube comments, Reddit threads, and Reddit reviews often reveal quality issues the description hides.
4. **Check the length**: Can you complete it within your available time?
5. **Check the format**: Do you learn better from text, video, or interactive exercises? Choose accordingly.

## Community-vetted resources for specific skills

See [platforms-communities.md](platforms-communities.md) for detailed breakdowns of each platform.

### For coding
- **Beginner**: Python.org tutorial (free), CS50 (Harvard/Youtube, free), freeCodeCamp Python
- **DSA**: LeetCode (free tier), CodeChef (free), CP-Algorithms (concept explanations)
- **Web dev**: The Odin Project (free), MDN Web Docs (reference), YouTube channels (freeCodeCamp, CodeWithHarry)
- **Advanced**: MIT OCW, Coursera specializations (audit for free)

### For design
- **UI/UX**: Figma (free), Google UX Design course (Coursera), YouTube (Malewicz, Femke)
- **Visual design**: Canva (free), Dribbble (inspiration), Material Design guidelines

### For math/olympiads
- **Foundation**: NCERT (class 11-12), AoPS (Art of Problem Solving) books
- **Practice**: AoPS forums, past olympiad papers (official HBCSE website)
- **Advanced**: Problem-Solving Strategies by Arthur Engel, IMO Shortlist

### For general skills
- **Communication**: YouTube pitch analysis, Toastmasters (free visits)
- **Project management**: Notion templates, YouTube
- **Research**: Google Scholar, Connected Papers, Elicit
