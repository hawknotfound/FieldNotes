# Platforms & Communities

Detailed guide to the most useful platforms and communities for Indian students.

---

## Opportunity Platforms

### Unstop
- **Website:** [unstop.com](https://unstop.com)
- **What it is:** India's largest student engagement platform
- **Lists:** Hackathons, competitions, internships, jobs, events
- **Why it matters:** Single source for most Indian student opportunities
- **Tips:** Create a profile, set alerts for your interests

### Devfolio
- **Website:** [devfolio.co](https://devfolio.co)
- **What it is:** Hackathon hosting and participation platform
- **Lists:** Online and offline hackathons, mostly tech-focused
- **Why it matters:** Many college hackathons use Devfolio for submissions

### Internshala
- **Website:** [internshala.com](https://internshala.com)
- **What it is:** Internship and training platform
- **Lists:** Internships, trainings, competitions
- **Why it matters:** Most popular internship platform for Indian students

### HackerEarth
- **Website:** [hackerearth.com](https://hackerearth.com)
- **What it is:** Coding challenges, hackathons, hiring assessments
- **Lists:** Hackathons, coding challenges, hiring challenges
- **Why it matters:** Many Indian companies use it for campus hiring

### Devpost
- **Website:** [devpost.com](https://devpost.com)
- **What it is:** Global hackathon platform
- **Lists:** Online hackathons, many with cash prizes
- **Why it matters:** Access to global hackathons, good prize pools

### MLH (Major League Hacking)
- **Website:** [mlh.io](https://mlh.io)
- **What it is:** Global student hackathon league
- **Lists:** Weekend hackathons worldwide
- **Why it matters:** Indian students can join online MLH events

## Learning Platforms

### freeCodeCamp
- **Website:** [freecodecamp.org](https://freecodecamp.org)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Best for:** Full-stack web development, hands-on

### The Odin Project
- **Website:** [theodinproject.com](https://theodinproject.com)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Best for:** Web development, project-based learning

### CSES Problem Set
- **Website:** [cses.fi/problemset](https://cses.fi/problemset)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Best for:** Algorithm practice, competitive programming preparation

### NPTEL
- **Website:** [nptel.ac.in](https://nptel.ac.in)
- **Cost:** Free (certificate at nominal fee)
- **Best for:** IIT-level course content, domain knowledge

### MIT OCW
- **Website:** [ocw.mit.edu](https://ocw.mit.edu)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Best for:** Advanced learning, MIT course materials

## Scholarship & Funding Platforms

### Buddy4Study
- **Website:** [buddy4study.com](https://buddy4study.com)
- **What it is:** India's largest scholarship platform
- **Lists:** Government and private scholarships

### Common Fellowship Portal
- **Website:** [fellowships.gov.in](https://fellowships.gov.in)
- **What it is:** Government of India centralized portal
- **Lists:** Research fellowships, doctoral programs

### National Scholarship Portal
- **Website:** [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)
- **What it is:** Government scholarship applications
- **Lists:** Central and state government scholarships

## Communication Platforms for Events

- **Discord** — most hackathons use Discord for communication
- **Slack** — some corporate events use Slack
- **Telegram** — many Indian student communities are on Telegram
- **WhatsApp** — college groups, event-specific groups

---

## Note on verifying information

Platforms and websites change. Before relying on any resource:
1. Check if the website is active (recent blog posts, events)
2. Look for recent student testimonials or reviews
3. Verify deadlines on official websites (not third-party pages)
4. When in doubt, contact the organizers directly
