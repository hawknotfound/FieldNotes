# Government Innovation Schemes

Key Government of India initiatives that support student innovation and entrepreneurship.

---

## Atal Innovation Mission (AIM)

- **Under:** NITI Aayog
- **Website:** [aim.gov.in](https://aim.gov.in)
- **Objective:** Create and promote a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship across India

### Key programs under AIM

#### Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL)
- **For:** Schools (grades 6-12)
- **Grant:** ₹20 lakh per school over 5 years
- **Purpose:** Establish maker spaces with 3D printers, robotics kits, sensors, etc.
- **Impact:** 10,000+ ATLs established, 1.1 crore+ students engaged
- **How to apply:** Through the school administration on the AIM portal

#### Atal Incubation Centres (AIC)
- **For:** Universities and institutions
- **Grant:** Up to ₹10 crore for setting up world-class incubators
- **Impact:** 70+ AICs established, 3,500+ startups supported
- **How students benefit:** AICs provide mentorship, seed funding, workspace, and industry connections

#### Atal New India Challenge (ANIC)
- **For:** Startups and MSMEs
- **Grant:** Up to ₹1 crore for solving national problems
- **Focus areas:** Healthtech, agritech, clean energy, water, education

#### Mentor of Change Program
- **For:** Professionals who want to mentor students
- **Impact:** 6,200+ mentors registered
- **How students use it:** Connect with mentors through ATLs and AICs

## Startup India

- **Under:** Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT)
- **Website:** [startupindia.gov.in](https://startupindia.gov.in)
- **Key benefits for students:**
  - Recognition as a startup (certificate)
  - Tax exemptions
  - Access to the Startup India Hub
  - Networking with investors and accelerators
  - Easy compliance norms

### Student Startup Policy
AICTE has a dedicated Student Startup Policy that encourages:
- Academic credit for startup work
- Gap year for students pursuing startups
- Incubation support in colleges
- Industry mentorship programs

## BIRAC Programs

- **Under:** Department of Biotechnology
- **Website:** [birac.nic.in](https://birac.nic.in)

| Program | Grant | For |
|---|---|---|
| Biotechnology Ignition Grant (BIG) | Up to ₹50 lakh | Early-stage biotech startups |
| LEAP | Up to ₹30 lakh | Women entrepreneurs |
| Seed Funding | Up to ₹25 lakh | Student biotech projects |

---

## MeitY Startup Hub

- **Under:** Ministry of Electronics and IT
- **Focus:** Electronics, IT, digital technologies
- **Programs:**
  - TIDE (Technology Incubation and Development of Entrepreneurs)
  - SAMRIDH (accelerator program)
  - Multiplier Grants Scheme (MGS) — up to ₹4 crore for joint R&D

---

## DST Innovation Programs

- **Under:** Department of Science and Technology
- **Website:** [dst.gov.in](https://dst.gov.in)

| Program | Purpose |
|---|---|
| INSPIRE Award | School-level innovation projects |
| NIDHI (National Initiative for Developing and Harnessing Innovations) | Incubation support |
| PRISM | Promoting innovations in science and mathematics |

## How students can access these schemes

1. **School level:** Talk to your school about setting up an ATL or participating in INSPIRE
2. **College level:** Join your institution's E-Cell, innovation club, or incubation center
3. **Individual level:** Apply for relevant programs directly through the official websites
4. **Key portals to bookmark:**
   - [aim.gov.in](https://aim.gov.in)
   - [startupindia.gov.in](https://startupindia.gov.in)
   - [birac.nic.in](https://birac.nic.in)
   - [dst.gov.in](https://dst.gov.in)
   - [meity.gov.in](https://meity.gov.in)
