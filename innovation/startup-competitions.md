# Startup Competitions

Major startup and innovation competitions relevant to Indian students.

---

## Hult Prize

- **Global student startup competition**
- **Annual prize:** $1,000,000 in seed funding
- **Theme:** Changes yearly, aligned with UN SDGs
- **Format:** Campus rounds → Regional finals → Global finals
- **Indian participation:** Very active, multiple Indian teams reach global finals
- **Website:** [hultprize.org](https://hultprize.org)

Hult Prize is open to all college students. You do not need an existing startup — just an idea and a team. It is the most well-funded student competition globally.

---

## Youth Co:Lab National Innovation Challenge

- **Organizers:** Atal Innovation Mission + UNDP + T-Hub Foundation
- **Focus:** Social innovation, sustainable development
- **Eligibility:** 15-29 years (extended to 35 for marginalized groups)
- **Prizes:** Seed grants, mentorship, incubation at T-Hub
- **Website:** [aim.gov.in/youthcolab.php](https://aim.gov.in/youthcolab.php)

---

## AWS Campus Fund Grand Challenge

- **Focus:** Student-led startups
- **Prizes:** $10,000 non-dilutive grants, up to ₹8 crore potential funding
- **Perks:** AWS credits ($25,000), software credits ($800,000 value)
- **Open to:** Student founders with registered startups
- **Website:** Check Campus Fund for announcements

---

## NASSCOM Start-up Accelerator Programs

NASSCOM runs multiple programs for student startups including:
- NASSCOM Startup Warehouse
- NASSCOM Zinnov Awards for innovation
- NASSCOM AI Gamechangers

These are more relevant for early-stage startups than ideas.

## IIM Business Plan Competitions

All major IIMs host annual business plan competitions:

| IIM | Competition | Typical Prize |
|---|---|---|
| Ahmedabad | Confluence B-Plan | ₹5-10 lakhs |
| Bangalore | Vista B-Plan | ₹3-5 lakhs |
| Calcutta | Intaglio B-Plan | ₹3-5 lakhs |
| Lucknow | Sangram B-Plan | ₹2-3 lakhs |
| Kozhikode | Compass B-Plan | ₹2-3 lakhs |

Open to all college students. Most require a detailed business plan submission.

## Eureka! (IIT Bombay)

- **Asia's largest business plan competition**
- **Part of:** IIT Bombay E-Cell
- **Prizes:** Cash + incubation + mentorship
- **Categories:** Multiple tracks for different stages
- **Website:** [ecell.in/eureka](https://ecell.in/eureka)

---

## Texas Instruments Innovation Challenge

- **Focus:** Embedded systems, electronics innovation
- **Prizes:** Cash + TI hardware
- **Eligibility:** Engineering students
- **Website:** [ti.com](https://ti.com)

---

## BIRAC BIG (Biotechnology Ignition Grant)

- **Focus:** Biotech, healthcare, medical devices
- **Grant:** Up to ₹50 lakhs
- **For:** Early-stage biotech startups
- **Website:** [birac.nic.in](https://birac.nic.in)

Not strictly a competition, but a government grant program that students with biotech innovations can apply for.

## Tips for winning

- Validate your idea before pitching (talk to potential users)
- Practice your pitch extensively
- Have a clear business model (revenue, costs, unit economics)
- Show traction — even a small number of users helps
- Know your numbers: TAM, SAM, SOM
- Prepare for hard questions about competition and risks
