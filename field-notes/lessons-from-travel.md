# Lessons from Travel: Field Notes

Real travel lessons learned from attending events in different Indian cities.

## Overnight trains are the student MVP

Sleeper class overnight trains save both money and time. You travel while you sleep. Compared to buses or flights, trains offer: more legroom, a bathroom, a place to charge your phone, and food available onboard. Book at least 2 weeks ahead for popular routes (tatkal is expensive and stressful).

## The booking mistake most students make

Booking a train that arrives 2 hours before the event starts. Trains in India run 1-4 hours late routinely. Always aim to arrive the night before. The extra night's accommodation cost is worth the peace of mind.

## Budget accommodation options that work

- Hostels (GoStops, Zostel, Hosteller) — ₹500-800/night, common in event cities
- Dorm rooms in student areas
- Airbnb shared rooms for groups of 3-4
- Some hackathons provide accommodation — check before booking anything
- College hostels of friends — most useful option if you know someone

## What to pack for travel

- Chargers and power bank (most important, most forgotten)
- A small bag, not a large suitcase — you will move around
- photocopy of your ID (Aadhaar/driving licence)
- Paracetamol, ORS packets, band-aids
- Face mask and small hand sanitizer
- A thin towel (many budget places do not provide clean ones)
- Earphones and eye mask for sleeping on trains

## Safety for solo travelers

- Share your live location with a family member or friend
- Use Ola/Uber for late-night travel, not random autos
- Keep the event venue address and emergency numbers written down
- In a new city, stay in well-known areas, not isolated budget places
- Most Indian cities are safe during events, but stay aware

## Learning about a city during an event stay

If you arrive a day early or leave a day late, explore the area around the venue. The 2-3 hours of exploration between event activities can give you a real feel for the city. Short visits shape your understanding more than any guide.

## Budget breakdown (real example)

A 3-day hackathon trip from Delhi to Bangalore:
- Train (Shatabdi 3AC): ₹2,200 (round trip)
- Accommodation (hostel, 2 nights): ₹1,600
- Food (3 days): ₹1,200
- Local transport: ₹800
- Contingency: ₹500
- Total: ~₹6,300

This is feasible with planning. Early booking and group travel can reduce it further.

## The return journey is the hardest

After 48 hours of hacking, the last thing you want is a 6-hour general-return bus. Spend a little extra on a comfortable return. Your body will thank you.
