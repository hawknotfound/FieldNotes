# Field Notes

Grounded observations from real hackathons, competitions, events, and travel — not theory, not motivation. What actually happens before, during, and after.

## Why this exists

Most guides tell you what to do. These notes tell you what it actually feels like — the boring parts, the confusing parts, the moments where things go wrong. If you have never been to an event, reading this will save you from surprises.

## Files in this folder

- [before-the-event.md](before-the-event.md) — Registration, preparation, travel planning, mental prep
- [during-the-event.md](during-the-event.md) — The actual experience: what happens hour by hour
- [after-the-event.md](after-the-event.md) — Follow-ups, reflection, applying what you learned
- [lessons-from-travel.md](lessons-from-travel.md) — Real travel lessons from attending events in different cities

## How to use this

1. If preparing for your first event, start with `before-the-event.md`
2. During the event, `during-the-event.md` helps you navigate common situations
3. After the event, `after-the-event.md` helps you maximize what you gained
4. `lessons-from-travel.md` is useful before booking any travel for an event

These are not rules. They are observations. Your experience will differ. That is the point.
