# Opportunity Evaluation Framework

A 9-category scoring system to evaluate any opportunity objectively. Score each category 1-5, then total.

Total out of 45. Use the score to decide: Apply, Maybe, or Skip.

## Scoring categories

### 1. Organizer credibility (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Government body (AIM, DST, MHRD), top university (IIT, IISc, etc.), or major multinational company (Google, Microsoft) |
| 4 | Known Indian company, reputable college, established community (GDG, MLH), or well-known platform (Unstop, Devfolio) |
| 3 | Young but credible organization. Has run 2+ past editions. Active social media with real engagement. |
| 2 | Unknown organizer. Minimal online presence. No past edition data. |
| 1 | Cannot identify organizer. Website is vague. No contact information. |

### 2. Learning value (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Structured mentorship, workshops from experts, hands-on project building, feedback sessions |
| 4 | Good workshops or talks + significant building time |
| 3 | Mix of learning and competition. Some guidance available. |
| 2 | Mostly listening to talks, very little hands-on work. |
| 1 | No learning component. Pure competition or exam. |

### 3. Networking value (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | High-quality participants (selected through competitive process), industry mentors, recruiters present |
| 4 | Good mix of peers from across India, some industry presence |
| 3 | Mostly local participants, some networking opportunities |
| 2 | Online event with minimal interaction |
| 1 | Solo participation, no interaction with others |

### 4. Travel value (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Event is in your city OR full travel + accommodation provided |
| 4 | Travel reimbursement provided, accommodation arranged |
| 3 | Moderate travel cost (under ₹2,000) and reasonable accommodation available |
| 2 | High travel cost (₹3,000-₹5,000) |
| 1 | Very high travel cost (₹5,000+) with no support. Event location is remote/difficult to reach. |

Note: For events in your own city, score this 4 or 5 by default (no travel burden).

### 5. Cost (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Free to apply and participate. All expenses covered. |
| 4 | Free to apply. You pay for travel/food only. |
| 3 | Free to apply. You pay for travel + accommodation. |
| 2 | Nominal participation fee (under ₹500) |
| 1 | Significant fee (₹500+) or application fee required |

### 6. Time commitment (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | 1 day or less. Weekend event. No prep needed. |
| 4 | 2-3 days. Moderate prep (5-10 hours). |
| 3 | 4-7 days. Significant prep (10-20 hours). |
| 2 | 1-2 weeks. Heavy prep (20+ hours). May conflict with classes. |
| 1 | 2+ weeks. Full-time commitment. Will definitely conflict with academics. |

### 7. Age suitability (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Designed exactly for your age group and academic level |
| 4 | Open to your level. Some participants may be older/younger but you fit. |
| 3 | You are at the lower or upper edge of eligibility |
| 2 | You barely meet eligibility. Most participants will be significantly older or younger. |
| 1 | You are not in the target age group but technically eligible through a loophole |

### 8. Safety (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Well-known venue (university campus, convention center). Organizer has safety protocols. Code of conduct published. |
| 4 | Reputable venue. Safety information available on request. |
| 3 | Moderate safety. Venue is in a known area. No code of conduct but no concerns reported. |
| 2 | Unfamiliar venue or area. Limited safety information. |
| 1 | Safety concerns reported by past participants. Venue in isolated or unsafe area. |

### 9. Uniqueness (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Once-in-a-year opportunity. National-level. Cannot get this experience elsewhere. |
| 4 | Unique but has similar alternatives. High value even if not one-of-a-kind. |
| 3 | Several similar events exist. This one is slightly better than average. |
| 2 | Many similar events available. This one is average. |
| 1 | Generic. Can get the same experience from a dozen other events. |

## Total score

| Total (out of 45) | Decision |
|---|---|
| 36-45 | **Apply.** Strong opportunity. High priority. |
| 27-35 | **Maybe.** Apply if you have time and energy. Compare with other options. |
| 18-26 | **Weak.** Only apply if you have nothing else going on or it is your first opportunity. |
| Below 18 | **Skip.** Your time is better spent elsewhere. |

## Weighting

Not all categories matter equally for everyone. Adjust weights:

- If you are a beginner: weight Learning value higher (x2)
- If you have budget constraints: weight Cost and Travel value higher (x2)
- If you have limited time: weight Time commitment higher (x2)
- If you are building a career: weight Organizer credibility and Networking value higher (x2)

Multiply the weighted categories by 2 before adding to the total. (Max weighted score: 45 + 5x5 = 70)

## Worked example: Smart India Hackathon

| Category | Score | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Organizer credibility | 5 | Government of India, MHRD |
| Learning value | 4 | 36-hour building time, mentor availability |
| Networking value | 4 | Participants from across India, industry judges |
| Travel value | 4 | Travel and accommodation provided for finalists |
| Cost | 5 | Free. Everything covered. |
| Time commitment | 3 | 36-hour event + prep time + travel |
| Age suitability | 4 | Open to all college students |
| Safety | 5 | College campuses, organized by government |
| Uniqueness | 5 | India's largest hackathon, once a year |
| **Total** | **39/45** | **Apply** |

## Worked example: Unknown online hackathon

| Category | Score | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Organizer credibility | 2 | Unknown platform, first edition, no track record |
| Learning value | 2 | Just building, no workshops or mentorship advertised |
| Networking value | 2 | Online, no structured interaction |
| Travel value | 5 | Online, no travel needed |
| Cost | 5 | Free |
| Time commitment | 4 | Weekend event, 2 days |
| Age suitability | 3 | Open to all, no specific targeting |
| Safety | 3 | Online, minimal risk |
| Uniqueness | 1 | Generic hackathon, hundreds like it |
| **Total** | **27/45** | **Maybe — apply only if you have free time** |

## Using this framework

1. Score each category honestly
2. Add up the total
3. Check the decision table
4. If "Maybe" and you have multiple options, compare scores side by side
5. If you are on the border (35-36), consider weighted scoring or your gut feeling
