# Decision Frameworks

Move from "what is this opportunity?" to "should I do this opportunity?"

Every framework here is designed to be used in under 10 minutes. Print them, bookmark them, run through them before every decision.

## How the frameworks connect

```
You find an opportunity
        ↓
[Red Flags & Green Flags] — quick safety check (2 min)
        ↓
[Is This Worth Applying For?] — 5-question pre-filter (2 min)
        ↓
[Opportunity Evaluation Framework] — full scoring (10 min)
        ↓
[Travel Worthiness Checklist] — if travel is involved (5 min)
        ↓
[Parent Discussion Checklist] — before talking to family (10 min)
        ↓
[Event Readiness Checklist] — before clicking register (5 min)
        ↓
You register with confidence
```

You do not need every framework for every decision. Use the ones relevant to your situation.

## When to use what

| Situation | Use |
|---|---|
| You found an opportunity but something feels off | red-flags-and-green-flags.md |
