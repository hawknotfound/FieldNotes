# Event Readiness Checklist

Before you click "Register" or confirm participation, verify every item below.

## Eligibility and fit

- [ ] I meet ALL eligibility criteria (class, age, marks, field, experience)
- [ ] The event dates do not clash with exams, family events, or other commitments
- [ ] I understand what the event expects from participants (prior skills, equipment, time)
- [ ] I have the required skills or am confident I can learn in the available time

## Registration

- [ ] Registration is open and the deadline is confirmed
- [ ] I have all required documents ready (ID, resume, portfolio, transcripts)
- [ ] Application fee (if any) is legitimate and budgeted — see [red-flags-and-green-flags.md](red-flags-and-green-flags.md)
- [ ] I know the selection process and timeline

## Travel (if applicable)

- [ ] Travel booked (train, bus, or flight) — see [travel/](../travel/index.md)
- [ ] Backup travel plan exists (alternative route if primary is delayed)
- [ ] Travel budget calculated and arranged
- [ ] I know how to reach the venue from the station/airport
- [ ] Offline maps downloaded

## Permissions

- [ ] Parents informed and consent given — see [parent-discussion-checklist.md](parent-discussion-checklist.md)
- [ ] College permission obtained if needed (some colleges require formal approval)
- [ ] I have emergency contacts saved (organizers, accommodation, family)

## Accommodation

- [ ] Accommodation confirmed (event-provided or self-arranged)
- [ ] If self-arranged: booking confirmed, location is safe, distance from venue is reasonable
- [ ] Check-in and check-out timings known

## Documents and equipment

- [ ] Laptop and charger packed
- [ ] Phone charger and power bank packed (10,000+ mAh)
- [ ] College ID and one additional ID proof (Aadhaar, driving licence)
- [ ] Event confirmation/ticket saved (offline and online)
- [ ] Printed resume (for networking)
- [ ] Notebook and pen

## Health and safety

- [ ] Basic first-aid items packed (paracetamol, ORS, band-aids)
- [ ] Any personal medication packed
- [ ] Water bottle packed
- [ ] Snacks packed (dry fruits, granola — no noisy or smelly food)
- [ ] I know the emergency procedures and contact numbers

## Team (if applicable)

- [ ] Team members confirmed
- [ ] Roles assigned (developer, designer, presenter)
- [ ] Communication channel set up (WhatsApp group, Discord)
- [ ] Team meetup plan agreed (where and when to meet)

## Preparation

- [ ] I have done basic preparation (learning relevant tools, reading problem statements, preparing project ideas)
- [ ] I have a rough plan for the event (what I want to learn, who I want to meet, what I want to build)
- [ ] I know the event schedule and key timings (inauguration, judging, closing)

## Go/No-go

If you answered NO to any of the following, do not register until it is resolved:

- Eligibility criteria (if you do not meet them, do not apply)
- Clash with exams (prioritize academics)
- Parental permission (if parents said no and you cannot resolve it)
- Travel not booked for offline events (prices rise closer to date)
- Accommodation not sorted for offline events

If all other checkboxes are checked or resolved, you are ready.

## After registering

- [ ] Confirmation email received and saved
- [ ] Calendar blocked for event dates
- [ ] WhatsApp/Telegram/Discord group joined (if shared by organizers)
- [ ] Pre-event tasks completed (if any — team formation, idea submission, document upload)
- [ ] Travel and accommodation finalized within 1 week of registration confirmation
