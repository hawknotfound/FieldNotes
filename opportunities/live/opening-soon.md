## 2. Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Organizer** | Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell (MIC) and AICTE |
| **Official Website** | [sih.gov.in](https://www.sih.gov.in) |
| **Registration Link** | Not yet available. Registration opens through college SPOC. |
| **Eligibility** | All undergraduate, postgraduate, and PhD students from recognized Indian institutions |
| **School Students?** | No (main SIH). There is a separate SIH Junior for school students. |
| **College Students?** | Yes |
| **Team Requirements** | 6 members per team (including at least 1 female participant), plus 1-2 mentors from the institution |
| **Location** | Multiple centers across India (grand finale) |
| **Online / Offline / Hybrid** | Online idea submission + offline grand finale |
| **Registration Fee** | Free |
| **Registration Expected** | July–August 2026 (based on previous years) |
| **Event Dates** | Expected August–December 2026 |
| **Expected Difficulty** | Medium. Problem statements range from easy to hard. Suitable for beginners in some tracks. |
| **Skills Required** | Varies by problem statement. Software edition: coding, web/mobile dev. Hardware edition: electronics, IoT, robotics. |
| **Focus / Theme** | Multiple themes: healthcare, education, smart cities, agriculture, etc. |
| **Prize Pool** | ₹1,00,000 per problem statement winner + national recognition |
| **Why It Might Be Worth Joining** | India's largest hackathon. National visibility. Government recognition. Winning can be career-defining. Free to participate. |
| **Potential Risks / Drawbacks** | Very high participation (50,000+ students). College SPOC must register the institution first. Team size of 6 is large and hard to coordinate. |
| **Notes** | **SIH 2025 happened Aug-Dec. SIH 2026 is expected on a similar timeline but not yet announced.** Registration happens through your college's SPOC (Single Point of Contact). Talk to your college innovation cell or TPO. |
