# Live Opportunities

Currently available and upcoming opportunities for Indian students — hackathons, innovation challenges, competitions, and ideathons.

**Last updated**: June 5, 2026

## How this is organized

| File | What it contains |
|---|---|
| [open-now.md](open-now.md) | Opportunities with open registration as of this date |
| [opening-soon.md](opening-soon.md) | Opportunities expected to open in the next 1-3 months |
| [recurring-annual.md](recurring-annual.md) | Events that happen every year with predictable timing |

## How to use this

1. Start with **open-now.md** if you want to apply for something immediately
2. Check **opening-soon.md** to plan 1-3 months ahead
3. Use **recurring-annual.md** to plan your year

## Important caveats

- Dates change. Deadlines get extended. Events get cancelled. Always verify on the official website.
- Registration links are provided when available. If a link is missing, search the event name + "2026" on Unstop or Google.
- "Expected" dates are based on past editions. They are not confirmed until the organizer announces them.
- If you find a broken link or closed registration, [open an issue](https://github.com/hawknotfound/FieldNotes/issues) or submit a correction.
