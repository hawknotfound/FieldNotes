# Opportunities

Curated list of fellowships, scholarships, and early-career programs relevant to Indian students.

## What this covers

- Student fellowships (Indian and international)
- Scholarships for study and research
- Early-career programs (internships, apprenticeships)
- Exchange programs
- Competitions that lead to direct opportunities

## Categories

| Type | Time commitment | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Fellowships | 1-12 months | Students interested in research, policy, social impact |
| Scholarships | Varied | Students needing financial support |
| Internship programs | 2-6 months | All students, especially years 2-3 |
| Exchange programs | 1-2 semesters | College students with good academics |
| Accelerator programs | 3-6 months | Student founders and startups |

## Key principle

Many opportunities are not widely advertised. The most competitive programs require planning 6-12 months in advance. The earlier you start looking, the more options you have.

## Opportunity discovery workflow

1. **Scan**: 15 minutes every Sunday on core platforms (Unstop, Devfolio, LinkedIn)
2. **Shortlist**: Pick 3-5 opportunities that match your eligibility, interest, and schedule
3. **Evaluate**: Score each using the framework in [how-to-evaluate.md](how-to-evaluate.md)
4. **Apply**: Focus on 2-3 applications at a time — quality over quantity
5. **Track**: Maintain a simple tracker (Notion, Google Sheets) with deadlines and status

See [how-to-discover.md](how-to-discover.md) for the full discovery system.

## Evaluation framework summary

When assessing an opportunity, consider:
- **Signal value**: Will this help your career or resume?
- **Learning value**: Will you grow from this experience?
- **Network value**: Who will you meet?
- **Opportunity cost**: What are you missing by doing this?
- **Logistics**: Can you actually make it work?

For the full 25-point scoring system, see [how-to-evaluate.md](how-to-evaluate.md).

## A note on accuracy

Programs change: eligibility criteria shift, deadlines move, and some programs are discontinued. Always verify details on the official website before applying. This guide is a starting point, not a definitive list.
