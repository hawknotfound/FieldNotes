# Fellowships & Scholarships

Notable programs available to Indian students at various stages.

---

## Fellowships for Indian Students

### PMRF (Prime Minister's Research Fellowship)
- **For:** PhD students in IITs, IISc, IISERs, central universities
- **Benefit:** ₹70,000-80,000/month + research grant
- **Eligibility:** Top performers in GATE/JEST/NET or direct admission
- **Website:** [pmrf.in](https://pmrf.in)

### Kishore Vaigyanik Protsahan Yojana (KVPY)
- **For:** Students in basic sciences (Class 11 to undergraduate)
- **Benefit:** ₹5,000/month (up to ₹20,000/month for different stages)
- **Eligibility:** Class 11/12 students, college students in science
- **Status:** Partially merged with INSPIRE (check current status)
- **Website:** [kvpy.iisc.ac.in](https://kvpy.iisc.ac.in)

### INSPIRE Fellowship (DST)
- **For:** Science students, PhD-track
- **Benefit:** ₹58,000/month + ₹2,00,000/year research grant
- **Eligibility:** Top 1% in Class 12 board exams (INSPIRE Award) or top rankers in NSE/INO
- **Website:** [online-inspire.gov.in](https://online-inspire.gov.in)

### Gandhi Fellowship
- **For:** Young leaders interested in social impact
- **Duration:** 2 years
- **Stipend:** ~₹50,000/month + benefits
- **Focus:** Education, rural development, government systems
- **Website:** [gandhifellowship.org](https://gandhifellowship.org)

### Young India Fellowship (YIF)
- **For:** Recent graduates
- **Duration:** 1-year postgraduate program at Ashoka University
- **Coverage:** Full tuition + stipend for selected candidates
- **Focus:** Liberal arts, leadership, interdisciplinary learning
- **Website:** [youngindiafellowship.com](https://youngindiafellowship.com)

### LAMP Fellowship (PRS Legislative Research)
- **For:** Young researchers interested in public policy
- **Duration:** 1 year
- **Stipend:** Competitive
- **Focus:** Working with Members of Parliament on legislative research
- **Website:** [prsindia.org](https://prsindia.org)

### Tata Innovation Fellowship
- **For:** Scientists and researchers in biotechnology
- **Benefit:** ₹1,00,000/month + research grant
- **Eligibility:** PhD holders with proven research record
- **Website:** [dbtindia.gov.in](https://dbtindia.gov.in)

## Scholarships for Indian Students

### National Means-Cum-Merit Scholarship
- **For:** Class 9-12 students
- **Benefit:** ₹12,000/year
- **Eligibility:** Family income < ₹3.5 lakh/year
- **Website:** [scholarships.gov.in](https://scholarships.gov.in)

### CBSE Udaan Scholarship
- **For:** Girl students in classes 11-12
- **Benefit:** Free coaching, tablets, study material
- **Focus:** Engineering preparation
- **Website:** [cbseudaan.nic.in](https://cbseudaan.nic.in)

### AICTE Pragati Scholarship
- **For:** Girl students in AICTE-approved colleges
- **Benefit:** ₹50,000/year (tuition + incidental)
- **Eligibility:** Family income < ₹8 lakh/year
- **Website:** [aicte-india.org](https://aicte-india.org)

### Fulbright-Nehru Fellowships
- **For:** Master's and PhD students (and researchers)
- **Coverage:** Tuition, living expenses, airfare, health insurance
- **Eligibility:** Indian citizens with strong academic record
- **Website:** [usief.org.in](https://usief.org.in)

### Commonwealth Scholarships
- **For:** Master's and PhD students from Commonwealth countries
- **Coverage:** Full tuition, living expenses, airfare
- **Eligibility:** Indian citizens (applications through AIU)
- **Website:** [cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk)

### Stanford Reliance Dhirubhai Fellowship
- **For:** Indian students admitted to Stanford MBA
- **Coverage:** Full tuition and living expenses
- **Eligibility:** Stanford GSB admit + demonstrated financial need
- **Website:** [gsb.stanford.edu](https://gsb.stanford.edu)

## Early Career Programs

### Google Summer of Code (GSoC)
- **For:** Students and open source contributors (18+)
- **Stipend:** ~$1,500-$3,300 (varies by country)
- **Duration:** 3 months
- **Focus:** Contributing to open source organizations
- **Website:** [summerofcode.withgoogle.com](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com)

### MLH Fellowship
- **For:** Students interested in open source and software engineering
- **Duration:** 12 weeks
- **Stipend:** Yes (paid)
- **Focus:** Open source contributions with mentorship
- **Website:** [mlh.io/fellowship](https://mlh.io/fellowship)

### Outreachy
- **For:** Students and others facing underrepresentation in tech
- **Stipend:** $7,000 (total)
- **Duration:** 3 months
- **Focus:** Open source contribution
- **Website:** [outreachy.org](https://outreachy.org)

## How to Apply Effectively

1. **Start early** — 6-12 months before the deadline
2. **Research past winners** — understand the profile they select
3. **Get strong recommendations** — build relationships with professors
4. **Write clearly** — essays and statements matter more than grades in many programs
5. **Apply to multiple programs** — do not rely on one
6. **Follow instructions exactly** — many applications are rejected for incomplete submissions
