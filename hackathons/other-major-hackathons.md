# Other Major Hackathons in India

Beyond Smart India Hackathon, several recurring hackathons are worth knowing about. This file covers the most prominent ones.

---

## Flipkart Grid

- **Organizer:** Flipkart
- **Focus:** Technology, software development, AI/ML
- **Format:** Online rounds + offline finale
- **Team size:** 2-3 members
- **Prizes:** Cash prizes, internship opportunities, Flipkart merchandise
- **Website:** [flipkartgrid.com](https://flipkartgrid.com)

Flipkart Grid typically has multiple tracks — software development, machine learning, and sometimes robotics. It is one of the most popular corporate hackathons for Indian engineering students.

---

## HSBC Hackathon

- **Organizer:** HSBC
- **Focus:** Fintech, banking technology, data science
- **Format:** Online + offline finale
- **Eligibility:** College students and recent graduates
- **Prizes:** Cash + internship/placement opportunities

HSBC runs multiple hackathons annually. They are good for students interested in finance and technology.

---

## TechGig Code Gladiators

- **Organizer:** TechGig (Times Internet)
- **Focus:** Competitive coding, multiple languages
- **Format:** Online only
- **Team size:** Individual or team
- **Prizes:** Cash, gadgets, recognition
- **Website:** [techgig.com/codegladiators](https://techgig.com/codegladiators)

One of India's largest coding competitions with significant participation.

---

## AngelHack

- **Organizer:** AngelHack
- **Focus:** General technology, social impact
- **Format:** Global hackathons with Indian editions
- **Prizes:** Cash, incubation opportunities, mentorship
- **Website:** [angelhack.com](https://angelhack.com)

AngelHack runs hackathons in multiple Indian cities. They focus on building products with social impact.

---

## Devfolio Season Hackathons

- **Organizer:** Devfolio
- **Focus:** Web3, blockchain, general tech
- **Format:** Online, often with bounty-based prize distribution
- **Prizes:** Cash bounties, grants
- **Website:** [devfolio.co](https://devfolio.co)

Devfolio hosts a variety of community hackathons throughout the year. They are beginner-friendly and often have good prize pools.

---

## Major League Hacking (MLH) Events

- **Organizer:** MLH
- **Focus:** Student hackathons, global
- **Format:** Weekend-long events (often online or hybrid)
- **Eligibility:** Students worldwide, including India
- **Prizes:** Swag, certificates, mentorship
- **Website:** [mlh.io](https://mlh.io)

MLH partners with universities globally. Indian students can join MLH events hosted by Indian universities or global online editions.

---

## Google-sponsored Hackathons

Google periodically sponsors hackathons in India through:
- Google Developer Groups (GDG)
- Google Cloud community events
- Build with AI series
- Google Solution Challenge

These are announced through the GDG network and Google's developer communities.

---

## Campus-specific hackathons

Most IITs, NITs, and major universities conduct annual hackathons as part of their technical festivals. Examples:

- **IIT Bombay** — Techfest hackathons
- **IIT Delhi** — Tryst hackathons
- **VIT Vellore** — Gravitas hackathons
- **SRM Chennai** — Aaruush hackathons

Check university fest websites and Unstop for listings.
