# Smart India Hackathon (SIH)

Smart India Hackathon is India's largest national hackathon, organized by the Ministry of Education in collaboration with AICTE. It invites students to solve problem statements provided by government ministries, departments, PSUs, and industries.

## Overview

- **Organizer:** Ministry of Education, AICTE
- **Frequency:** Annual
- **Format:** Software and Hardware editions
- **Team size:** 6 members + 1-2 mentors
- **Duration:** 36-hour grand finale (after initial screening)
- **Website:** [sih.gov.in](https://www.sih.gov.in)

## Timeline (typical)

| Stage | Timeframe |
|---|---|
| Problem statement submission by ministries | April-May |
| Problem statement release | June-July |
| College-level registration | July-August |
| Internal hackathon / idea submission | August-September |
| Grand finale (shortlisted teams) | September-December |
| Result announcement | Within 2-4 weeks after finale |

Exact dates vary each year. Check the official website for current timelines.

## Eligibility

- Students enrolled in Indian higher education institutions (recognized by AICTE/UGC)
- Both undergraduate and postgraduate students
- Interdisciplinary teams allowed
- Junior edition available for school students

## Problem statements

Problem statements are submitted by:
- Central and state government ministries
- PSUs (Public Sector Undertakings)
- Industries and NGOs

Themes cover sectors such as:
- Agriculture, FoodTech, Rural Development
- Healthcare, MedTech, Biotech
- Smart Automation, AI/ML
- Smart Vehicles, Transportation
- Education, Cybersecurity, Blockchain
- Sustainability, Clean Energy
- Finance, Banking

## Selection process

1. **Problem statement selection** — teams choose from published PSs
2. **Internal hackathon** — colleges conduct internal rounds to select teams
3. **Idea submission** — shortlisted teams submit detailed solutions
4. **Grand finale** — selected teams build prototypes at physical nodal centers
5. **Evaluation** — judged by experts from the sponsoring ministry/industry

## Prizes

- Winning teams: ₹1,00,000 per team
- Runner-up teams: ₹75,000 per team
- All participants receive certificates
- Winning solutions may be deployed by the sponsoring organization

## How to prepare

1. **Form a diverse team** — include developers, a designer, a presenter
2. **Understand the problem deeply** — read the PS document carefully
3. **Research existing solutions** — know what already exists
4. **Plan your tech stack** — use familiar technologies
5. **Prepare a pitch** — practice presenting your solution clearly
6. **Test before the finale** — ensure your prototype works

## Tips

- SIH is judged on innovation, feasibility, impact, and presentation
- A working prototype beats a polished demo that does not work
- Government problem statements often require practical, scalable solutions
- Read the evaluation criteria published by the organizing ministry
- Network with mentors and judges — they are industry experts
