# Hackathons

Hackathons are time-bound events where participants collaborate to build solutions — usually software or hardware prototypes — for specific problem statements or themes. In India, hackathons range from national government initiatives to campus-level events.

## Why hackathons matter

- Build project experience outside coursework
- Learn to work under pressure and in teams
- Get exposure to real-world problem statements from industry and government
- Network with peers, mentors, and recruiters
- Win prizes, recognition, and sometimes job offers
- Strengthen your portfolio and resume

## Types of hackathons in India

| Type | Example | Typical Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Government-led national hackathons | Smart India Hackathon | 36 hours (grand finale) |
| Corporate hackathons | Flipkart Grid, HSBC Hackathon | 24-48 hours |
| College fest hackathons | IIT Techfest, college fests | 24-36 hours |
| Online platform hackathons | Devfolio, HackerEarth, Unstop | 1-7 days |
| Community hackathons | MLH Localhost, GDG events | 1-2 days |

## Key platforms to find hackathons

- [Unstop](https://unstop.com) — largest Indian student competition platform
- [Devfolio](https://devfolio.co) — popular for student hackathons
- [HackerEarth](https://hackerearth.com) — coding challenges and hackathons
- [Devpost](https://devpost.com) — global hackathon listings
- [MLH](https://mlh.io) — Major League Hacking, global student hackathons
- [Internshala Competitions](https://internshala.com/competitions) — curated Indian competitions

## Notes

- Most hackathons are free to participate in
- Team composition matters: a mix of developers, designers, and presenters works best
- Many hackathons accept beginners and provide mentors
- Online hackathons are more accessible for first-timers
