# Career Path: Designer

Creating visual and interactive experiences for digital products.

## What this looks like

Design in India is growing fast. UI/UX designers are in demand at startups, product companies, and agencies.

## Typical trajectory

1. Learn design principles (color, typography, layout)
2. Learn Figma (industry standard tool)
3. Redesign existing apps (practice)
4. Build a portfolio of case studies
5. Freelance or intern
6. Full-time UI/UX designer or product designer

## Specializations

| Role | Focus |
|---|---|
| UI Designer | Visual design, interfaces |
| UX Designer | User research, flows, usability |
| Product Designer | End-to-end design |
| Visual Designer | Brand, graphics, illustration |
| Motion Designer | Animations, micro-interactions |

## Skills needed

| Skill | Priority |
|---|---|
| Figma | Essential |
| Design principles | Essential |
| User research | Important |
| Prototyping | Important |
| Basic HTML/CSS | Good to have |

## India context

- Design salaries: ₹3-8 LPA (fresher), ₹8-15 LPA (2-4 years)
- Remote freelance is common via Upwork, Fiverr
- Bangalore, Delhi, Mumbai have most design jobs
- Indian startups value designers who also understand business

## Where to start

- Figma is free for students (get edu plan)
- Follow design communities (Dribbble, Behance)
- Redesign 3 apps you use daily
- Read: Don't Make Me Think, Refactoring UI
- Join designer communities on Twitter and LinkedIn
- Contribute design work to open source projects
