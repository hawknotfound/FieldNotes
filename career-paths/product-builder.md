# Career Path: Product Builder

Designing, building, and shipping digital products.

## What this looks like

Product roles sit between tech, business, and design. You figure out what to build and why. Product management (PM) is the most common entry point.

## Typical trajectory

1. Build side projects (shows initiative)
2. Learn basic tech skills (enough to talk to developers)
3. Learn user research and problem validation
4. Intern as an APM (Associate Product Manager)
5. Full-time PM or founder

## Skills needed

| Skill | Priority |
|---|---|
| Communication | Essential |
| Problem-solving | Essential |
| Basic tech literacy | Important |
| User research | Important |
| Data analysis | Important |
| Wireframing | Good to have |

## India context

- APM roles exist at Flipkart, Swiggy, Ola, Razorpay, and startups
- PM is a common path to startups and VC
- Indian PM salaries: ₹4-12 LPA (fresher), ₹15-25 LPA (2-4 years)
- Strong overlap with founder skills

## Where to start

- Ship a small product (website, app, tool)
- Write about your process (blog, Twitter)
- Read: Lenny's Newsletter, Ravi Mehta's writing
- Join product communities (ProductHunt, GrowthX)
- Analyze products you use daily — what works, what does not
