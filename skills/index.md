# Skills

Notes on building practical skills that help students participate in hackathons, competitions, and real-world projects.

## Why skills matter

- Most hackathons and competitions require more than textbook knowledge
- Employers and selectors look for demonstrated ability, not just marks
- Building projects teaches you more than any course

## Key skill areas for Indian students

| Skill | Why | Where to start |
|---|---|---|
| Programming | Hackathons, coding competitions, projects | Python, JavaScript, C++ |
| Web development | Build and deploy projects fast | HTML/CSS/JS, React, Node.js |
| UI/UX design | Make your projects look professional | Figma (free), basic design principles |
| Data structures & algorithms | Coding competitions, tech interviews | CP-Algorithms, LeetCode |
| Communication & pitching | Present your work effectively | Practice, feedback, recording yourself |
| Project management | Deliver on time in team settings | Notion, Trello, basic planning |
| Research skills | Understand domains you build for | Google Scholar, papers, blogs |

## How to approach skill-building

1. **Learn by doing** — build projects while learning
2. **Focus on one thing at a time** — depth before breadth
3. **Use free resources** — YouTube, documentation, open courses
4. **Build in public** — share your progress on LinkedIn/Twitter
5. **Teach others** — explaining solidifies understanding
