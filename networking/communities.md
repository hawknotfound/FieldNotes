# Indian Student Communities

Active communities where Indian students can learn, network, collaborate, and find opportunities.

---

## Tech Communities

### GDG (Google Developer Groups)
- Local city-based groups across India
- Host workshops, hackathons, study jams
- Free to join
- [gdg.community.dev](https://gdg.community.dev)

### DSC / GDSC (Google Developer Student Clubs)
- College-based clubs
- Focus on Google technologies
- Lead roles available for students
- Check if your college has an active chapter

### The Elites
- Student-run tech community
- Hosts hackathons (prize pool ₹2L+)
- Active on WhatsApp, Instagram
- [theelites.in](https://theelites.in)

### HackLoop
- Student tech community focused on collaborative building
- Hosts events, sessions, and hackathons
- [hackloop.me](https://hackloop.me)

### CodeChef Chapters
- College-based competitive programming chapters
- Organize coding contests and workshops
- [codechef.com](https://codechef.com)

### Mozilla Campus Clubs
- Open source focused
- Privacy, web literacy, open innovation
- Active in many Indian colleges

### Hackathon Communities

| Community | Purpose | Platform |
|---|---|---|
| HackWithIndia | India's biggest hackathon community | Discord/Website |
| Devfolio Community | Hackathon builders | Discord |
| MLH Community | Global student hackathons | Discord/Slack |

## Student-run collectives

### Student Developer Community (SDC)
- Student-run, multiple college chapters
- Workshops, hackathons, mentorship

- Active Discord with 50k+ members

### FOSS United

- Monthly meetups in Bangalore, Delhi, Mumbai, Pune
- [fossunited.org](https://fossunited.org)

## Professional Networks

### LinkedIn
- Most important professional platform for Indian students
- Follow companies, people, and pages in your field
- Post about your projects, learnings, and event experiences
- Connect meaningfully (not random connection requests)

### Twitter (X)
- Tech Twitter in India is very active
- Follow #Hackathon, #CompetitiveProgramming, #100DaysOfCode
- Engage with builders and creators

### GitHub
- Show your work through projects
- Contribute to open source
- Your GitHub profile is your resume for tech roles

### Dev.to / Medium
- Write about what you learn
- Indian tech audience is active on both platforms
- Good for building writing skills and personal brand

## Finding Communities Near You

- Search "GDG [your city]" on Google or Meetup
- Search "GDSC [your college]" on LinkedIn
- Look for communities on Campus Diaries (Unstop)
- Check [Meetup.com](https://meetup.com) for Indian tech meetups
- Join WhatsApp groups (search using Unstop, LinkedIn)

---

## Tips for Joining Communities

1. **Lurk first, then engage** — observe the culture before posting
2. **Introduce yourself** — a short intro in the #introductions channel
3. **Be helpful** — answer questions when you can
4. **Attend meetups** — virtual or in-person
5. **Take leadership roles** — become a club lead, event organizer, or mentor
6. **Stay active** — consistency matters more than intensity
