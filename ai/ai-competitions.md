# AI Competitions for Students

AI-focused competitions where Indian students can participate, learn, and win.

---

## Kaggle Competitions

- **Platform:** [kaggle.com/competitions](https://kaggle.com/competitions)
- **Format:** Data science and ML challenges
- **Typical timeline:** 1-3 months
- **Prizes:** Cash (some competitions), Kaggle medals, recognition
- **Skill level:** Beginner to advanced

Kaggle is the most popular ML competition platform. Start with "Getting Started" competitions (Titanic, House Prices) before moving to featured competitions.

**For Indian students:** Kaggle has a large Indian community. Many competitions have cash prizes in USD.

---

## International AI Olympiad (IOAI)

- **Organizer:** ACM India
- **Focus:** AI and machine learning for school students
- **Pathway:** India AI Olympiad (INAIO) → IOAI
- **Website:** [india.acm.org/education/inaio](https://india.acm.org/education/inaio)

India AI Olympiad is new (recently launched by ACM India). It is the pathway to the International AI Olympiad. Check the website for current timelines and registration.

---

## AI for Education Hackathon (Wadhwani AI)

- **Focus:** Building AI solutions for education problems in India
- **Format:** Offline hackathon
- **Special:** Winning solutions get piloted in Indian schools
- **Platform:** [Reskilll](https://reskilll.com)

---

## Google Gemini / AI Hackathons

Google periodically runs AI-focused hackathons through:
- Google Cloud community events
- Google Developer Groups (GDG)
- Build with AI series

These are announced on the GDG network. Follow local GDG chapters.

## AI Competitions on Unstop

- **Website:** [unstop.com](https://unstop.com)
- **Filter:** Search for AI/ML competitions
- **Types:** Corporate hackathons, college competitions, case studies
- **Examples:** Flipkart Grid (has ML tracks), HSBC Hackathon (has data science tracks)

## How to Prepare for AI Competitions

| Competition Type | How to Prepare |
|---|---|
| Kaggle | Learn pandas, scikit-learn, basic ML |
| AI Hackathons | Build quick prototypes, use APIs |
| AI Olympiad | Study ML theory, practice problems |
| Startup comps | Validate idea, practice pitch |

---

## Building a Portfolio in AI

Instead of just doing competitions, build projects that show your skills:

1. **A deployed AI project** (Streamlit app, Hugging Face Space)
2. **A blog post** explaining what you built
3. **A GitHub repo** with clean code and documentation
4. **Competition results** on Kaggle or similar platforms
5. **Open source contributions** to AI libraries

Projects > certificates for most opportunities.
