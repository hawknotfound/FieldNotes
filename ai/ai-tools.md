# AI Tools — Curated for Students

A practical, grounded list of AI tools worth knowing about.

---

## LLMs / Chat Interfaces

| Tool | Best for | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | General use, coding, writing | Free tier, Plus $20/mo |
| Claude (Anthropic) | Long documents, thoughtful writing | Free tier, Pro $20/mo |
| Gemini (Google) | Google integration, research | Free tier, Advanced $20/mo |
| DeepSeek | Coding, long context, free | Free |
| Perplexity | Research with citations | Free tier, Pro $20/mo |
| Grok (xAI) | Real-time info, X integration | Free with X account |

**Student tip:** Master one tool deeply rather than switching between all of them. Use the free tiers — they are powerful enough for most tasks.

## Important Notes

- **Free tiers are often enough** for student projects and learning
- **The GitHub Student Developer Pack** includes Copilot, Namecheap, DigitalOcean credits — get it if you are a student
- **Do not pay for AI tools** unless you have a specific need that the free tier cannot meet
- **Check if your college provides access** — some institutions have enterprise agreements
- **API costs** for small projects are usually under ₹500-1000
